Anywhere in the run

A Shell Game guide to the characters and altars that follow you through a run

Checks that follow you

Most of Shell Game's checks belong to one area. These don't. Sparrow and Beg are characters who show up when you've done something to earn their attention, and Kali's altars appear on ordinary levels all through the run. Their checks happen wherever the game puts them, so they're listed here rather than on an area's page.

In Shell Game each of these checks holds whatever your seed put there, not the item the base game gives.

  • Sparrow
  • Beg
  • Kali

Sparrow

Character · Shoplifter, Idol Heist, Tusk's Vault

What it is

Sparrow is a thief, and she notices other thieves. Rob a shop early in the run and she seeks you out, then pulls you into a plan to rob Madame Tusk, which runs from the Tide Pool to Neo Babylon. Shell Game has three checks along the way:

  • Shoplifter, the gift she gives you when you first meet;
  • Idol Heist, stealing Madame Tusk's Idol in the Tide Pool;
  • Tusk's Vault, the haul at the end of the plan in Neo Babylon.

How to reach it

Sparrow only appears if you've stolen from a shop. Once you have, she turns up on a later level before Olmec's Lair. The rest of her quest takes place in the Tide Pool and in Neo Babylon.

Shoplifter

Check · Sparrow's first meeting

The first time you meet Sparrow, she gives you a gift for your nerve. In the base game it's a pile of ropes. In Shell Game it's this check's reward.

How to reach it

Rob a shop in the Dwelling, the Jungle or Volcana, then carry on. Sparrow finds you on one of the levels that follow, before you reach Olmec's Lair.

How to do it

  1. Pick up something in a shop and leave with it without paying. The shopkeeper comes after you with a shotgun, so be ready to run or fight.
  2. Keep going. When Sparrow appears, go up to her and let her talk.
  3. Pick up the reward she leaves you.

A robbery has a price: shopkeepers stay angry with you for the rest of the run.

Idol Heist and Tusk's Vault

After your first meeting, Sparrow asks you to meet her in the Tide Pool, where Madame Tusk keeps her Idol behind a locked door. Stealing it is the Idol Heist check. Carry Madame Tusk's Idol through a level exit and the plan moves on: Sparrow waits for you in Neo Babylon, at Tusk's Vault.

In Shell Game the Idol Heist holds a check's reward, and Madame Tusk's Idol is shuffled to a check somewhere in the run. It's the Idol itself that moves Sparrow's plan on, wherever you find it, and Shell Game always makes it available by Neo Babylon. The Tide Pool and Neo Babylon pages walk through both checks.

Beg

Character · Angering Kali, You Love Chaos

What it is

Beg is a wandering devotee of chaos who admires anyone willing to defy Kali. Destroy one of her altars and he comes to congratulate you; do it again and he comes back with a bigger present. Each visit is a check.

How to reach it

Beg only appears after you've destroyed a Kali altar, and he turns up on a later level. Kali's altars appear on ordinary levels throughout the run. Bombs break them, as does any other explosion.

Kali punishes anyone who wrecks her altar, so be ready for it, and collect any of her checks you want from that altar first.

Angering Kali

Check · Beg's first visit

Beg's first visit, after you destroy your first altar. In the base game he gives you a bag of bombs to keep the chaos going. In Shell Game he gives you this check's reward.

How to reach it

Destroy a Kali altar, then carry on to the levels that follow.

How to do it

  1. Find a Kali altar and blow it up with a bomb.
  2. Survive Kali's anger and keep going.
  3. When Beg appears, go up to him and let him talk, then pick up the reward he leaves you.

You Love Chaos

Check · Beg's second visit

Beg's second visit, after you've met him once and destroyed another altar. In the base game he gives you the True Crown. In Shell Game he gives you this check's reward, and the True Crown is shuffled to a check somewhere in the run.

How to reach it

Do Angering Kali first. This check needs Beg's first visit behind you.

How to do it

  1. After meeting Beg, destroy another Kali altar.
  2. Keep going until Beg appears again, go up to him, and take the reward he leaves you.

With Shell Game's True Crown Restoration option on, once Beg's second visit is over Kali's curse is lifted, if she has cursed you, and you're restored to 4 health.

Kali

Altars · Giving Gifts, Sacrifice, Making Kali Happy

What it is

Kali is the goddess whose altars you'll find all through the run: a stone slab beneath a statue of her. Lay a creature on the slab and Kali takes it, and you earn her favor. Favor builds up over the whole run, across every altar you use, and Kali rewards you when it's high enough. Shell Game has three checks at her altars: two of her rewards for favor, and a Present that Shell Game leaves out for you to give her.

Shell Game also changes what her altar in the Duat does. Those changes are options, described under Options on the Shell Game page.

How to reach it

Kali's altars turn up on ordinary levels in most areas. Any altar counts toward these checks, and your favor carries from one altar to the next.

Giving Gifts

Check · the Present at Kali's altar

Shell Game wraps up a Present for Kali. Give it to her at her altar and she leaves this check's reward in its place. In the base game a Present given to Kali turns into an Eggplant.

How to reach it

The first time you reach a level that has both a Kali altar and a pet (a dog, a cat or a hamster), Shell Game turns the pet into a Present. If the pet was for sale in a shop, the Present is too, at the same price.

How to do it

  1. Find the Present where the pet would have been, and pick it up. If it's in a shop, buy it.
  2. Carry it to the altar and set it down on the slab.
  3. Kali takes it and leaves the reward on the altar.

Don't open the Present or break it anywhere else: you'll get what's inside, not the check. You can't take a Present out of its level either. If it's lost, Shell Game wraps up a new one on the next level that has an altar and a pet.

Sacrifice

Check · Kali's first gift

The first time your favor with Kali is high enough, she drops a gift at her altar. In the base game it's one of a handful of items. In Shell Game it's this check's reward.

How to reach it

Any Kali altar. Giving her the Present doesn't use up this check.

How to do it

  1. Find a body to offer: an enemy, a cave dweller, anything that lived. Stun it rather than kill it if you can: Kali values a living sacrifice above a dead one.
  2. Carry or throw it onto the altar's slab, and wait for Kali to take it.
  3. Keep offering sacrifices until Kali drops her first gift, then pick up the reward.

Making Kali Happy

Check · Kali's second gift

Keep earning favor after her first gift and Kali gives you a second one. In the base game it's the Kapala. In Shell Game it's this check's reward, and the Kapala is shuffled to a check somewhere in the run.

How to reach it

Any Kali altar, after you've done Sacrifice.

How to do it

  1. Keep offering sacrifices after Kali's first gift, the same way as before.
  2. When your favor is high enough, Kali drops her second gift. Pick up the reward.
