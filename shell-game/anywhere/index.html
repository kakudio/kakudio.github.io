Anywhere in the run

A Shell Game guide to the characters and altars that follow you through a run, and to the items it shuffles

Checks that follow you

Most of Shell Game's checks belong to one area. These don't. Sparrow and Beg are characters who show up when you've done something to earn their attention, and Kali's altars appear on ordinary levels all through the run. Their checks happen wherever the game puts them, so they're listed here rather than on an area's page.

In Shell Game each of these checks holds whatever your seed put there, not the item the base game gives.

Sparrow

Character · Shoplifter, Idol Heist, Tusk's Vault

What it is

Sparrow is a thief, and she notices other thieves. Rob a shop early in the run and she seeks you out, then pulls you into a plan to rob Madame Tusk, which runs from the Tide Pool to Neo Babylon. Shell Game has three checks along the way:

  • Shoplifter, the gift she gives you when you first meet;
  • Idol Heist, stealing Madame Tusk's Idol in the Tide Pool;
  • Tusk's Vault, the haul at the end of the plan in Neo Babylon.

How to reach it

Sparrow only appears if you've stolen from a shop. Once you have, she turns up on a later level before Olmec's Lair. The rest of her quest takes place in the Tide Pool and in Neo Babylon.

Shoplifter

Check · Sparrow's first meeting

The first time you meet Sparrow, she gives you a gift for your nerve. In the base game it's a pile of ropes. In Shell Game it's this check's reward.

How to reach it

Rob a shop in the Dwelling, the Jungle or Volcana, then carry on. Sparrow finds you on one of the levels that follow, before you reach Olmec's Lair.

How to do it

  1. Pick up something in a shop and leave with it without paying. The shopkeeper comes after you with a shotgun, so be ready to run or fight.
  2. Keep going. When Sparrow appears, go up to her and let her talk.
  3. Pick up the reward she leaves you.

A robbery has a price: shopkeepers stay angry with you for the rest of the run.

Idol Heist and Tusk's Vault

After your first meeting, Sparrow asks you to meet her in the Tide Pool, where Madame Tusk keeps her Idol behind a locked door. Stealing it is the Idol Heist check. Carry Madame Tusk's Idol through a level exit and the plan moves on: Sparrow waits for you in Neo Babylon, at Tusk's Vault.

In Shell Game the Idol Heist holds a check's reward, and Madame Tusk's Idol is shuffled to a check somewhere in the run. It's the Idol itself that moves Sparrow's plan on, wherever you find it, and Shell Game always makes it available by Neo Babylon. The Tide Pool and Neo Babylon pages walk through both checks.

Beg

Character · Angering Kali, You Love Chaos

What it is

Beg is a wandering devotee of chaos who admires anyone willing to defy Kali. Destroy one of her altars and he comes to congratulate you; do it again and he comes back with a bigger present. Each visit is a check.

How to reach it

Beg only appears after you've destroyed a Kali altar, and he turns up on a later level. Kali's altars appear on ordinary levels throughout the run. Bombs break them, as does any other explosion.

Kali punishes anyone who wrecks her altar, so be ready for it, and collect any of her checks you want from that altar first.

Angering Kali

Check · Beg's first visit

Beg's first visit, after you destroy your first altar. In the base game he gives you a bag of bombs to keep the chaos going. In Shell Game he gives you this check's reward.

How to reach it

Destroy a Kali altar, then carry on to the levels that follow.

How to do it

  1. Find a Kali altar and blow it up with a bomb.
  2. Survive Kali's anger and keep going.
  3. When Beg appears, go up to him and let him talk, then pick up the reward he leaves you.

You Love Chaos

Check · Beg's second visit

Beg's second visit, after you've met him once and destroyed another altar. In the base game he gives you the True Crown. In Shell Game he gives you this check's reward, and the True Crown is shuffled to a check somewhere in the run.

How to reach it

Do Angering Kali first. This check needs Beg's first visit behind you.

How to do it

  1. After meeting Beg, destroy another Kali altar.
  2. Keep going until Beg appears again, go up to him, and take the reward he leaves you.

With Shell Game's True Crown Restoration option on, once Beg's second visit is over Kali's curse is lifted, if she has cursed you, and you're restored to 4 health.

Kali

Altars · Giving Gifts, Sacrifice, Making Kali Happy

What it is

Kali is the goddess whose altars you'll find all through the run: a stone slab beneath a statue of her. Lay a creature on the slab and Kali takes it, and you earn her favor. Favor builds up over the whole run, across every altar you use, and Kali rewards you when it's high enough. Shell Game has three checks at her altars: two of her rewards for favor, and a Present that Shell Game leaves out for you to give her.

Shell Game also changes what her altar in the Duat does. Those changes are options, described under Options on the Shell Game page.

How to reach it

Kali's altars turn up on ordinary levels in most areas. Any altar counts toward these checks, and your favor carries from one altar to the next.

Giving Gifts

Check · the Present at Kali's altar

Shell Game wraps up a Present for Kali. Give it to her at her altar and she leaves this check's reward in its place. In the base game a Present given to Kali turns into an Eggplant.

How to reach it

The first time you reach a level that has both a Kali altar and a pet (a dog, a cat or a hamster), Shell Game turns the pet into a Present. If the pet was for sale in a shop, the Present is too, at the same price.

How to do it

  1. Find the Present where the pet would have been, and pick it up. If it's in a shop, buy it.
  2. Carry it to the altar and set it down on the slab.
  3. Kali takes it and leaves the reward on the altar.

Don't open the Present or break it anywhere else: you'll get what's inside, not the check. You can't take a Present out of its level either. If it's lost, Shell Game wraps up a new one on the next level that has an altar and a pet.

Sacrifice

Check · Kali's first gift

The first time your favor with Kali is high enough, she drops a gift at her altar. In the base game it's one of a handful of items. In Shell Game it's this check's reward.

How to reach it

Any Kali altar. Giving her the Present doesn't use up this check.

How to do it

  1. Find a body to offer: an enemy, a cave dweller, anything that lived. Stun it rather than kill it if you can: Kali values a living sacrifice above a dead one.
  2. Carry or throw it onto the altar's slab, and wait for Kali to take it.
  3. Keep offering sacrifices until Kali drops her first gift, then pick up the reward.

Making Kali Happy

Check · Kali's second gift

Keep earning favor after her first gift and Kali gives you a second one. In the base game it's the Kapala. In Shell Game it's this check's reward, and the Kapala is shuffled to a check somewhere in the run.

How to reach it

Any Kali altar, after you've done Sacrifice.

How to do it

  1. Keep offering sacrifices after Kali's first gift, the same way as before.
  2. When your favor is high enough, Kali drops her second gift. Pick up the reward.

Shuffled items

Every item Shell Game shuffles can turn up at any check, so each one's entry lives on this page. The full list of items, and when the progression items become available, is under How Shell Game works.

Each entry says what the item does, where the base game normally hands it out, and what it's for. In Shell Game that base-game place is a check like any other, and the item itself can be at any check in the run.

Progression items

Items · the ones a route needs

These open the checks and places the way to the Cosmic Ocean runs through. Shell Game always makes each one available before the check or place that needs it, as listed under How Shell Game works, so a seed never asks you for an item you can't have yet.

Udjat Eye

Progression item · available before the Black Market and Vlad's Castle

What it does. An eye you keep once you've picked it up. It blinks when you're near a hidden entrance in the second area, and it's the key to the two secrets there.

What it's needed for. In the Jungle it blinks as you near the rock that hides the Black Market; break through to the doorway. In Volcana, carry it to the drill at the top of the level to start the dig down to Vlad's Castle, and to Vlad above it.

Where the base game gives it. In the gold-key chest on 1-2 or 1-3, the Udjat Eye chest check.

Skeleton Key

Progression item · available before the locked room at Madame Tusk's Dice House

What it does. A key that opens locked doors and isn't used up, so you can carry it from one lock to the next. It takes up your hands like any key: throw it ahead of you when you need to climb or fight.

What it's needed for. The locked room beside Madame Tusk's Dice House in the Tide Pool, the Idol Heist check.

Where the base game gives it. Nowhere fixed: it's a rare find in shops and crates, and now and then in a pile of bones.

Excalibur

Progression item · available before the Kingu fight

What it does. A sword you swing in place of your whip. One swing kills most things.

What it's needed for. Kingu, in Abzu. Nothing hurts her until her shell cracks, and one swing of Excalibur cracks it. Before you set off Abzu's trap on 4-3, leave the sword somewhere safe higher up the level: whatever you're holding when you die there is lost.

Where the base game gives it. In the stone above the Great Lake on 4-2, which only gives it up to someone wearing the Crown or the Hedjet: the Excalibur's stone check.

Scepter

Progression item · available before the City of Gold

What it does. Anubis's staff, carried in your hands. With the Crown or the Hedjet on your head, it opens the golden door on 4-2 in the Temple.

What it's needed for. The door to the City of Gold, and through it the Duat, where Anubis II and Osiris are.

Where the base game gives it. Anubis drops it on 4-1.

Ankh

Progression item · available before the Kingu and Osiris boss fights

What it does. Carry it and the next time you die, you come back to life. It's used up when it does.

What it's needed for. Two secret levels that can only be reached by dying. On 4-3 in the Tide Pool, the trap that drains the lava from Abzu's door kills whoever sets it off, and the Ankh brings you back to go through it to Kingu. In the Temple, being sacrificed with the Ankh on the bull altar in the City of Gold takes you to the Duat, where Osiris is.

Don't spend it by accident. It goes on any death, so a fall onto spikes on the way to 4-3 or the City of Gold uses it up. It won't save a Sun Challenge either: dying in that arena fails it, Ankh or not.

Where the base game gives it. At the top of the back layer behind Olmec's Lair, reached by riding Olmec's head down into the lava: the Olmec's Ankh check.

Crown

Progression item · available before the Kingu and Osiris boss fights

What it does. A crown you wear. It marks you as royalty: the stone in the Tide Pool gives Excalibur up to you, the golden door in the Temple opens for you with the Scepter, and shopkeepers give you a discount. The Hedjet does the same job at the stone and the door, so you need one or the other, not both.

What it's needed for. Excalibur's stone on the way to Kingu, and the door to the City of Gold on the way to Osiris.

Where the base game gives it. On the statue at the top of Vlad's Castle in Volcana.

Hedjet

Progression item · available before the Kingu and Osiris boss fights

What it does. The white crown of the pharaohs, worn on your head. It counts as a crown at the stone in the Tide Pool and at the golden door in the Temple, exactly as the Crown does.

What it's needed for. Excalibur's stone and the door to the City of Gold, if you don't have the Crown.

Where the base game gives it. It's the priciest thing for sale in the Jungle's Black Market.

Alien Compass

Progression item · available before the Mothership and the Lahamu fight

What it does. A compass you keep once you've picked it up. In the Ice Caves its arrow points to the Mothership's hidden entrance.

What it's needed for. Finding the Mothership, and Lahamu inside it. The entrance is a single-tile ledge over the Abyss at the bottom of 5-1, in a different place every run. Line yourself up with the arrow before you drop, because missing the ledge means falling into the Abyss.

Where the base game gives it. Van Horsing hands it over in the Temple, if you freed him in Volcana and he was there when Vlad died: the Van Horsing's reward check.

Eggplant

Progression item · available by the Ice Caves

What it does. A purple eggplant you carry in your hands. It breaks easily, so set it down rather than throw it, and keep it out of fights; a hired hand can carry it for you.

What it's needed for. The Eggplant Child. In the Ice Caves, set it on the altar beside the stone Moai head, and the Eggplant Child inside joins you. Keep the child alive to 7-1 and it opens the door to Eggplant World.

Where the base game gives it. Kali turns a Present into one when you lay it on her altar. In the base game the Present comes from Great Humphead in the Tide Pool; in Shell Game, Kali's Present is the Giving Gifts check.

Tablet of Destiny

Progression item · available by Neo Babylon

What it does. A stone tablet you keep once you've picked it up. In Neo Babylon it tells you which of the many Ushabti statues on 6-2 is the right one.

What it's needed for. The way up to the Sunken City. Carry the Ushabti the tablet marks from 6-2 to 6-3 and it hatches into the Qilin, a flying mount (see Neo Babylon). In Tiamat's Throne, once she's beaten, ride the Qilin up past the lasers to the Sunken City. Shell Game counts the tablet as part of every winning route.

Where the base game gives it. Whichever boss you beat at the end of the fourth area: Kingu in Abzu or Osiris in the Duat.

Madame Tusk's Idol

Progression item · available by Neo Babylon

What it does. A heavy golden idol that fills your hands. On its own it's treasure; to Sparrow it's proof you robbed Madame Tusk.

What it's needed for. Sparrow's quest, and its last check, Tusk's Vault. Carry the Idol out through a level exit and the quest moves on, wherever you found it. Keep it away from Madame Tusk and her bodyguard: if they see you with it, they attack.

Where the base game gives it. In the locked room by Madame Tusk's Dice House in the Tide Pool: the Idol Heist check.

Hou Yi's Bow

Progression item · available before Hundun

What it does. A bow you carry in your hands. Walk over an arrow while holding it and the bow picks the arrow up and loads it; attack to shoot it straight ahead, the way you're facing.

What it's needed for. With the Arrow of Light, the way into the Cosmic Ocean at Hundun. In Shell Game the bow comes on its own, without the Metal Arrow the base game puts with it.

Using it against Hundun.

  1. Carry the bow and the arrow into 7-4, and load the arrow before the fight: drop it, then walk over it with the bow in your hands.
  2. Kill both of Hundun's heads. When it falls and opens its one big eye, it can't hurt you any more, so there's no hurry.
  3. Stand level with the eye and face it, then shoot. Ignore the door that opens at the top of the level: that's the way to the ordinary ending.
  4. If you miss, the arrow drops where it stopped. Pick it up with the bow again and take another shot, and don't let it fall anywhere you can't follow.

Where the base game gives it. It's the prize of the Moon Challenge, in the Jungle or Volcana.

Arrow of Light

Progression item · available before Hundun

What it does. A single glowing arrow. Shot from Hou Yi's Bow into Hundun's open eye, it opens a portal to the Cosmic Ocean. There's only one, so look after it: lose it and the Cosmic Ocean stays shut for the run.

What it's needed for. Hundun, at the end of the Sunken City. How to load it and shoot it is under Hou Yi's Bow.

Where the base game gives it. It's the prize of the Sun Challenge in the Sunken City.

Capes and packs

Gear · worn on your back

The rest of the pool is gear: no route needs it, but all of it helps. You wear one back item at a time; picking up another swaps it for the one you have. Shell Game gives the Queen Bee, whom some runs never meet, only gear like this.

Cape

Gear · back item

What it does. Hold jump while you're falling and the cape spreads, so you drift down slowly and land without hurting yourself.

What it's good for. Long drops, and the Stars Challenge in the Tide Pool or the Temple: Shell Game always places a Cape, Vlad's Cape or the Jetpack before it.

Where the base game gives it. Shops and crates.

Vlad's Cape

Gear · back item

What it does. Everything the Cape does, plus a second jump in mid-air: press jump again at the top of a jump to go higher.

What it's good for. Getting up to places you couldn't otherwise reach, and the Stars Challenge in the Tide Pool or the Temple.

Where the base game gives it. Vlad drops it at the top of his castle.

Jetpack

Gear · back item

What it does. Hold jump in the air and it lifts you. It runs out of fuel after a few seconds and refills when you land, so fly in short hops from one footing to the next.

What it's good for. Going straight up, crossing gaps, and the Stars Challenge in the Tide Pool or the Temple.

Where the base game gives it. Anubis II drops one in the Duat. Shops and crates sell them too.

Telepack

Gear · back item

What it does. A Teleporter you wear. Jump, then press jump again in the air while holding a direction, and you jump a few tiles that way in an instant.

Using it. Look before you teleport. Landing inside rock kills you; landing inside an enemy kills the enemy instead, which is how a Telepack finishes Lahamu or Tiamat in one go. Stand right under the boss and teleport up into it.

Where the base game gives it. Shops and crates.

Powerpack

Gear · back item

What it does. Powers up your attacks: your whip sets things on fire, and your bombs blow up bigger.

What it's good for. Fighting. A bigger blast clears more rock, and reaches a boss from further away.

Where the base game gives it. Shops and crates.

Hoverpack

Gear · back item

What it does. Lets you hang in the air. It can't lift you, but it can keep you where you are for as long as you like.

Using it.

  1. Jump from solid ground, and press jump again at the top of the jump. You stop falling and float.
  2. While you float, move left or right to drift across. You keep the height you started at, so jump from as high as you can.
  3. Press jump again to stop hovering and drop. You can start and stop on the way down to come down in steps.

It's at its best over water, lava and spikes, and across wide gaps.

Where the base game gives it. Shops and crates.

Crowns and health

Gear · kept once you pick it up, or used at once

Kapala

Gear · kept

What it does. A cup made from a skull, which catches the blood of what you kill. Each time it fills, it empties into you and gives you back 1 health.

What it's good for. Staying alive through a long run. Fight rather than avoid the small enemies, and it keeps topping you up.

Where the base game gives it. Kali's second gift at her altar, the Making Kali Happy check.

Elixir

Gear · used at once

What it does. A potion that heals you as you pick it up, and cures poison and Kali's curse.

What it's good for. Getting out of trouble, and lifting Kali's curse if your dealings with her altars have earned you one.

Where the base game gives it. The prize of the Temple's Stars Challenge.

Eggplant Crown

Gear · worn · also the name of a check

What it does. A crown of eggplant leaves. It swaps your whip for an endless supply of eggplants to throw.

What it's good for. Fun, mostly, and a ranged attack that never runs out. It isn't the Eggplant that opens Eggplant World.

Where the base game gives it. The Eggplant King drops it at the top of Eggplant World: the Eggplant Crown check.

True Crown

Gear · worn

What it does. Every 22 seconds it teleports you a short way in the direction you're facing, as a Teleporter would, and gives you a stack of bombs each time.

Using it. You don't choose when it goes off, so count. As the 22 seconds come round, face open space, never a wall: landing inside rock kills you. Landing inside an enemy kills the enemy.

Where the base game gives it. Beg's second visit, after you've broken two of Kali's altars: the You Love Chaos check. With Shell Game's True Crown Restoration option on, that visit lifts Kali's curse and restores you to 4 health, whatever Beg gives you.

Royal Jelly

Gear · used at once

What it does. Restores 6 health as you pick it up.

What it's good for. A big heal before a hard fight. If you're already healthy, consider leaving it where it is until you need it, as long as you're coming back that way.

Where the base game gives it. The Queen Bee drops it, and there's one in Madame Tusk's Palace.

Shoes, gloves and other gear

Gear · kept once you pick it up

None of these take up a hand or your back. Once picked up, they work for the rest of the run.

Spike Shoes

Gear · kept

What it does. Jumping on things does far more damage, and hurts bosses that a plain jump can't, such as Quillback, the Queen Bee and Anubis.

Where the base game gives it. The Yeti Queen drops a pair. Shops and crates have them too.

Spring Shoes

Gear · kept

What it does. You jump much higher.

Where the base game gives it. Shops and crates.

Climbing Gloves

Gear · kept

What it does. Jump at a wall and you cling to it; you can climb up or down it and jump off again.

Where the base game gives it. Shops and crates.

Pitcher's Mitt

Gear · kept

What it does. What you throw flies straight and fast instead of arcing. With empty hands you can catch things thrown at you.

Where the base game gives it. Shops and crates.

Paste

Gear · kept

What it does. Your bombs stick to the first thing they touch, wall, floor or monster, and stay there until they go off.

What it's good for. Bosses. A stuck bomb can't roll away, which makes short work of the Queen Bee, Anubis and Osiris, and keeps bombs on Kingu's back.

Where the base game gives it. Shops and crates.

Spectacles

Gear · kept

What it does. Lets you see the gold and gems buried in the rock, so you know where it's worth digging.

Where the base game gives it. Shops and crates.

Compass

Gear · kept

What it does. An arrow at the edge of the screen points to the level's exit. It isn't the Alien Compass, and won't find the Mothership.

Where the base game gives it. The Yeti King drops one. Shops and crates have them too.

Tools and weapons

Gear · carried in your hands

Each of these fills your hands, so you can't carry another item at the same time. Set it down to pick something else up.

Mattock

Gear · carried

What it does. Swing it to dig through rock and earth. It breaks after a limited number of swings.

What it's good for. Getting into places without bombs, like the doorway to the Black Market.

Where the base game gives it. Shops and crates. Tun also lends you one for the Moon Challenge in the Jungle or Volcana, which Shell Game leaves as it is.

Teleporter

Gear · carried

What it does. Use it and you jump a few tiles in an instant, the way you're facing, or straight up if you hold up.

Using it.

  • Look before you use it. Landing inside rock kills you.
  • Landing inside an enemy kills it outright. Stand right under Lahamu or Tiamat, keep as close to the ground as you can, and teleport up into it. If it misses, try again.
  • It can carry you across a force field without breaking it, which is how you get into Tusk's Vault without angering Madame Tusk.

Where the base game gives it. Shops and crates.

Clone Gun

Gear · carried

What it does. A gun with three shots. Whatever you shoot with it, item or creature, gets a copy beside it. It's gone once the third shot is used.

What it's good for. Copying something worth having twice: a bag of bombs and ropes, or a Royal Jelly.

Where the base game gives it. The prize of the Tide Pool's Stars Challenge.

Freeze Ray

Gear · carried

What it does. Shoots a beam that freezes what it hits in a block of ice. A frozen enemy can't move or fight back, and can still be hurt.

What it's good for. Bosses. Frozen, Vlad can't teleport away, and Tiamat goes down to two blows to her frozen head.

Where the base game gives it. Shops and crates.

Shotgun

Gear · carried

What it does. Fires a spray of pellets that does heavy damage up close. Each shot knocks you back, so don't fire it standing at the edge of a drop.

What it's good for. Bosses with a lot of health, like Great Humphead and Tiamat.

Where the base game gives it. Shops, and every shopkeeper carries one. Kill a shopkeeper and theirs is yours, at the cost of every shopkeeper's anger for the rest of the run.

Plasma Cannon

Gear · carried

What it does. Fires a ball of plasma that explodes on what it hits, tearing through rock and monsters alike.

Using it. Its shots bounce off force fields and off Olmec straight back at you, so break a force field before you fire through it, and leave it alone against Olmec.

Where the base game gives it. Behind the force fields in the Mothership.

Bombs and ropes

Gear · added to your supplies

Picking one of these up adds its bombs or ropes to what you're carrying, and that's the end of it.

Bomb Box

Gear · bombs

What it does. A crate of 12 bombs, four times what a Bomb Bag holds.

Where the base game gives it. The gift for saving all of the Three Sisters. Shops sell them too.

Bag of ropes

Gear · ropes

What it does. A bag of 24 ropes.

Where the base game gives it. Sparrow's reward for splitting Tusk's Vault with her.

Bag of bombs and ropes

Gear · bombs and ropes

What it does. A stitched bag of 24 bombs and 24 ropes.

Where the base game gives it. Winning the Sun Challenge, alongside its prize: the Sun Challenge supplies check.
