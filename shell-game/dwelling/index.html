Dwelling

A guide to the first area of every run, for players of Shell Game

The Dwelling

The Dwelling is levels 1-1 to 1-4, the start of every run. Two of its middle levels hide something in the back layer, a locked chest and Yang's turkey pen, and 1-4 ends with its boss, Quillback.

At the end of 1-4 there are two exits: the left one leads to the Jungle, the right one to Volcana.

  • The Udjat Eye chest
  • Yang's turkeys and treasure
  • Quillback

The Udjat Eye chest

Either 1-2 or 1-3 has a small wooden hut in the back layer, reached through a door in the background near the main path. Inside is a locked chest holding the Udjat Eye, and the same level has a gold key lying somewhere in the front layer.

  1. Find the gold key and pick it up. You carry it in your hands, so you can't hold anything else at the same time; throw it ahead of you when you need to climb or fight, and keep it away from lava and spikes.
  2. Take the key through the back-layer door to the chest. The chest can't be moved, so the key has to come to it.
  3. Touch the chest with the key to open it, and take what's inside.

Yang's turkeys and treasure

On 1-2 or 1-3, a door into the back layer leads to a wooden pen with Yang standing by it, and a locked treasure room beside it. His turkeys have got out, and he wants two of them back in the pen. Bring them and he gives you the key to the treasure room. Finish it without upsetting him and he turns up again, friendly, later in the run.

Cave turkeys wander the front layer of the same level. Any two turkeys count, not only the ones the level starts with, and a third does nothing more.

  1. Catch a turkey. You can pick it up and carry it, or jump on its back and ride it.
  2. Take it through the back-layer door and leave it inside the pen.
  3. Do the same with a second turkey. Yang thanks you and throws you the key.

Yang turns on you if you:

  • attack him or steal from him;
  • kill a turkey where he can see it, or let him see a dead turkey or a cooked one, whoever is to blame;
  • take a turkey back out of the pen once it's in;
  • break any wooden part of the pen, so keep bombs well away from it;
  • open the treasure room with a key before both turkeys are in. He takes that as a robbery.

Stunning a turkey on the way, or bumping it about, doesn't count against you.

The treasure room

Pick up the key Yang tosses to you and open the door to his treasure room. It holds gold and gems, and sometimes a chest or a crate.

In Shell Game An extra item waits at the room's locked door, on top of the treasure that's already there. That item is Yang's Treasure on the map.

Quillback

The Dwelling's boss is a huge caveman who curls into a ball and rolls through the walls. Every 1-4 has him, waiting in his own walled arena low in the level, with ladders leading down into it and the level's two exits beyond it, one on each side. When he dies he drops a Bomb Bag and a cooked turkey.

  1. Come in from above and stay near a ladder. When he spots you he leaps toward you, and if you're far enough away he rolls instead, smashing the floor and walls in his path. Touching him while he rolls kills you outright, so get up a ladder or out of his line.
  2. Hurt him with things other than your whip, which does nothing to him, as does jumping on him unless you're wearing Spike Shoes. A bomb set down beside him just after a roll ends works well, as does one thrown so it goes off as it reaches him. Rocks, pots, skulls and arrows thrown at him all do damage too, one hit at a time.
  3. He has 10 health. When he dies, pick up his drop where he fell.

You don't have to fight him to leave the Dwelling: tempt him into rolling toward the exit you want, and follow the tunnel he makes. You give up his drop if you do.
