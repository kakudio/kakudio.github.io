A Shell Game guide to the checks in the first area of every run

The Dwelling's checks

The Dwelling is levels 1-1 to 1-4, the start of every run. In Shell Game each check here holds whatever your seed put there, not the item the base game gives, so a check can be worth doing even when you know the base game's prize.

At the end of 1-4 there are two exits: the left one leads to the Jungle, the right one to Volcana.

  • Udjat Eye chest
  • Yang's Treasure
  • Yang's quest
  • Quillback

Udjat Eye chest

Check · 1-2 or 1-3, back layer

What it is

A locked chest tucked away in the Dwelling's back layer. In the base game it always holds the Udjat Eye. In Shell Game it holds this check's reward instead, and the Udjat Eye is shuffled to a check somewhere in the run.

How to reach it

The chest turns up on either 1-2 or 1-3, in a small wooden hut in the back layer, reached through a door in the background near the main path. The same level has a gold key lying somewhere in the front layer.

How to do it

  1. Find the gold key and pick it up. You carry it in your hands, so you can't hold anything else at the same time; throw it ahead of you when you need to climb or fight, and keep it away from lava and spikes.
  2. Take the key through the back-layer door to the chest. The chest can't be moved, so the key has to come to it.
  3. Touch the chest with the key to open it, and take the reward. A Skeleton Key opens it too, if your seed gave you one already.

Yang's Treasure

Check · 1-2 or 1-3, back layer

What it is

Yang keeps a turkey pen in the back layer with a locked treasure room beside it. In the base game the room holds only gold and gems, and sometimes a chest or a crate. Shell Game adds this check's reward at the room's locked door, on top of the treasure that's already there.

How to reach it

Yang's pen is on 1-2 or 1-3, through a door into the back layer. The treasure room stays locked until he gives you its key.

How to do it

  1. Finish Yang's quest by putting two turkeys in his pen.
  2. Pick up the key Yang tosses to you and open the door to his treasure room.
  3. Take the reward, and the gold while you're there.

Don't open the door with a key of your own before the turkeys are in. Yang takes that as a robbery.

Yang's quest

Quest · 1-2 or 1-3

What it is

Yang's turkeys have got out, and he wants two of them back in his pen. Bring them and he gives you the key to his treasure room, which is the Yang's Treasure check. Finish it without upsetting him and he turns up again, friendly, later in the run.

How to reach it

On 1-2 or 1-3, look for a door into the back layer leading to a wooden pen with Yang standing by it. Cave turkeys wander the front layer of the same level. Any two turkeys count, not only the ones the level starts with, and a third does nothing more.

How to do it

  1. Catch a turkey. You can pick it up and carry it, or jump on its back and ride it.
  2. Take it through the back-layer door and leave it inside the pen.
  3. Do the same with a second turkey. Yang thanks you and throws you the key.

Yang turns on you if you:

  • attack him or steal from him;
  • kill a turkey where he can see it, or let him see a dead turkey or a cooked one, whoever is to blame;
  • take a turkey back out of the pen once it's in;
  • break any wooden part of the pen, so keep bombs well away from it;
  • open the treasure room with a key before both turkeys are in.

Stunning a turkey on the way, or bumping it about, doesn't count against you.

Quillback

Check · boss of 1-4

What it is

The Dwelling's boss: a huge caveman who curls into a ball and rolls through the walls. In the base game he drops a Bomb Bag and a cooked turkey when he dies. In Shell Game the Bomb Bag is replaced by this check's reward.

How to reach it

Every 1-4 has him. He waits in his own walled arena low in the level, with ladders leading down into it. The level's two exits are beyond it, one on each side.

How to do it

  1. Come in from above and stay near a ladder. When he spots you he leaps toward you, and if you're far enough away he rolls instead, smashing the floor and walls in his path. Touching him while he rolls kills you outright, so get up a ladder or out of his line.
  2. Hurt him with things other than your whip, which does nothing to him, as does jumping on him unless you're wearing Spike Shoes. A bomb set down beside him just after a roll ends works well, as does one thrown so it goes off as it reaches him. Rocks, pots, skulls and arrows thrown at him all do damage too, one hit at a time.
  3. He has 10 health. When he dies, pick up the reward where he fell.

You don't have to fight him to leave the Dwelling: tempt him into rolling toward the exit you want, and follow the tunnel he makes. You give up this check if you do.
