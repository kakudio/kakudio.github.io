Eggplant World

Eggplant World is a secret, peaceful level that takes the place of 7-2 in the Sunken City: purple water, pagodas and harmless eggplant creatures, with a king on a throne at the top. Its door on 7-1 stays shut unless you've carried an Eggplant Child all the way there, a quest that starts back in the Ice Caves.

Its one check is the Eggplant Crown. In Shell Game the check holds whatever your seed put there, not the Crown.

The Eggplant Child

Quest · Ice Caves to 7-1 · needs the Eggplant

What it is

The way into Eggplant World. A stone Moai head in the Ice Caves holds the Eggplant Child, who follows you like a hired hand. Delivered alive to a statue on 7-1, the child opens the door to Eggplant World. Shell Game leaves this quest as it is in the base game; what it changes is where the Eggplant comes from.

How to reach it

You need the Eggplant. In the base game you'd make one by offering a Present to Kali, but in Shell Game the Eggplant is shuffled to a check somewhere in the run, and Shell Game always makes it available by the Ice Caves. It arrives wrapped in a Present, so open the Present to get it out; that Present is not the Giving Gifts one, so don't take it to Kali. Once it's out, the Eggplant breaks easily if you drop or throw it, so carry it carefully, or have a hired hand carry it for you.

How to do it

  1. Find the Moai, a huge stone head, near the top of its level in the Ice Caves.
  2. Set the Eggplant on the purple-stained altar beside it. The Moai opens, and the Eggplant Child inside wakes up and joins you.
  3. Keep the child alive through the rest of the Ice Caves, Neo Babylon and Tiamat and up into the Sunken City. It follows you through each exit as a hired hand does.
  4. On 7-1, find the statue of a mother with open arms beside a sealed red door. Put the child in her arms and the door opens. It leads to Eggplant World in place of 7-2.

Eggplant Crown

Check · Eggplant World · needs the Eggplant

What it is

The Eggplant King sits on his throne at the top of Eggplant World. He can't hurt you, and he goes down to a single hit. In the base game he drops the Eggplant Crown, which swaps your whip for an endless supply of eggplants to throw. In Shell Game he drops this check's reward instead, and the Eggplant Crown is shuffled to a check somewhere in the run.

How to reach it

You have to get into Eggplant World first, through the Eggplant Child quest. Once you're in, climb to the top of the level. Nothing that lives here attacks you.

How to do it

  1. Climb to the throne. The King talks to you as you come near.
  2. Hit him in the head. One hit with anything that does damage is enough.
  3. Pick up the reward. He also leaves a pile of giant food, which heals you as you touch it, so top up your health before you go on.
  4. The level's exit is at the top, by the throne, and takes you back to the Sunken City.
