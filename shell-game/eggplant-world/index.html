Eggplant World

A guide to Eggplant World, for players of Shell Game

Eggplant World

Eggplant World is a secret, peaceful level that takes the place of 7-2 in the Sunken City: purple water, pagodas and harmless eggplant creatures, with a king on a throne at the top. Nothing that lives here attacks you.

Its door on 7-1 stays shut unless you've carried an Eggplant Child all the way there, a quest that starts back in the Ice Caves and ends with the Eggplant King's crown.

The Eggplant Child, and the Eggplant King

The way into Eggplant World is a child. A stone Moai head in the Ice Caves holds the Eggplant Child, who follows you like a hired hand once freed, and delivered alive to a statue on 7-1 opens the door to Eggplant World.

It all starts with an Eggplant, which the base game has you make by offering a Present to Kali. The Eggplant breaks easily if you drop or throw it, so carry it carefully, or have a hired hand carry it for you.

In Shell Game The Eggplant is shuffled, and always somewhere you can get it by the Ice Caves. It arrives wrapped in a Present, so open the Present to get it out. That Present is not the Giving Gifts one, so don't take it to Kali.

  1. Find the Moai, a huge stone head, near the top of its level in the Ice Caves.
  2. Set the Eggplant on the purple-stained altar beside it. The Moai opens, and the Eggplant Child inside wakes up and joins you.
  3. Keep the child alive through the rest of the Ice Caves, Neo Babylon and Tiamat and up into the Sunken City. It follows you through each exit as a hired hand does.
  4. On 7-1, find the statue of a mother with open arms beside a sealed red door. Put the child in her arms and the door opens. It leads to Eggplant World in place of 7-2.

The Eggplant King

The Eggplant King sits on his throne at the top of Eggplant World. He can't hurt you, and he goes down to a single hit. He drops the Eggplant Crown, which swaps your whip for an endless supply of eggplants to throw.

  1. Climb to the throne. The King talks to you as you come near.
  2. Hit him in the head. One hit with anything that does damage is enough.
  3. Pick up the Crown. He also leaves a pile of giant food, which heals you as you touch it, so top up your health before you go on.
  4. The level's exit is at the top, by the throne, and takes you back to the Sunken City.
