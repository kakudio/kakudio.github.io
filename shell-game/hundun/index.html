Hundun's Hideaway

Hundun's Hideaway is level 7-4, straight after 7-3 in the Sunken City, and it is where every Shell Game run is headed. There are no checks here. Instead this is where the items the mod moved around pay off: beat Hundun, then use Hou Yi's Bow and the Arrow of Light to open the way to the Cosmic Ocean.

Every seed has a way to bring both items here: Shell Game checks the whole layout before your run starts, and always makes the Bow and the Arrow available before Hundun.

Hundun

Boss · 7-4 · the way to the Cosmic Ocean

What it is

A giant creature that climbs up from the bottom of its level after you, sprouting a bird's head and then a snake's head on the way. Beating it ends the run with the game's second ending. If you have Hou Yi's Bow and the Arrow of Light, beating it also lets you open a portal to the Cosmic Ocean, the run of levels from 7-5 to 7-99, and that is the goal Shell Game sets every run.

How to reach it

Finish 7-3 of the Sunken City and Hundun is waiting on the next level. Before you go:

  • Pick up Hou Yi's Bow wherever your seed put it. In the base game it's the prize of the Moon Challenge, in the Jungle or Volcana; in Shell Game it can be at any check.
  • Pick up the Arrow of Light the same way. In the base game it's the Sun Challenge's prize.
  • Carry both into 7-4. Walking over the Arrow with the Bow in hand loads it.

Without both, you can still beat Hundun for the second ending, but the Cosmic Ocean stays shut.

How to do it

  1. The climb. Hundun rises from below and keeps coming, so keep climbing ahead of it. Its legs and body hurt to touch. Once it reaches the top of the level it grows wings and flies at you.
  2. The heads. On the way up, a bird's head bursts out of its shell, and a little higher a snake's head. Both spit fireballs that set you alight; hitting a head just before it spits stops that fireball, and water puts the fireballs out. The heads are the only parts you can hurt: kill both. Bombs are the quick way to do it.
  3. The eye. With both heads dead, Hundun falls and opens one big eye. It can't hurt you any more, and a door opens at the top of the level, the way to the second ending.
  4. The Cosmic Ocean. Instead of taking that door, shoot the open eye with the Arrow of Light from Hou Yi's Bow. A portal to the Cosmic Ocean opens. Step through it.

Look after the Arrow through the fight. There's only one, and if it's lost, the Cosmic Ocean stays shut for this run.
