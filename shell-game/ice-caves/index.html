The Ice Caves' checks

The Ice Caves are a single, very tall level, 5-1. Every run comes through them, whichever way it went after Olmec: from the Tide Pool or the Temple, and from their hidden areas too. The level leads on to Neo Babylon.

The level comes in three parts. You start at the top, among floating platforms of ice. Partway down, a doorway leads into the yetis' cave, where the Yeti Queen and Yeti King live. Below that is the stretch with the exit, and under it the Abyss, with the entrance to the Mothership hidden at its very bottom.

In Shell Game each check here holds whatever your seed put there, not the item the base game gives.

Yeti Queen

Check · boss · 5-1, the yetis' cave

What it is

A huge yeti who rules the upper half of the yetis' cave with a crowd of ordinary yetis around her. In the base game she drops a pair of Spike Shoes and a pile of rope when she dies. In Shell Game the Spike Shoes are replaced by this check's reward, and the Spike Shoes are shuffled to a check somewhere in the run.

How to reach it

Work your way down the top part of the level until you find the doorway into the yetis' cave. Go in. The Queen paces the upper floor of the cave, and the Yeti King is on the floor below her.

The cave is optional: with a Cape, Climbing Gloves or a Jetpack you can drop past it to the exit, so come in on purpose if you want her check.

How to do it

She has 10 health, can't be stunned, and jumping on her does nothing unless you're already wearing Spike Shoes. Stand on her head and she punches up at you, then throws herself at you while you're knocked about. When she spots you, she lets out a yell and leaps at you, landing with a slam that throws you, items and nearby yetis around.

  • Keep your distance and hurt her from range: bombs, a mine, a Shotgun, or the bodies of the yetis around her, thrown at her.
  • Or bomb the floor out from under her. She falls to the King's floor, where his breath may freeze her, and bombing the floor under both of them drops them onto the spikes at the bottom of the cave.

Pick up the reward where she died. You can leave the cave the way you came in if you'd rather not take on the King.

Yeti King

Check · boss · 5-1, the yetis' cave

What it is

The Yeti Queen's partner, as big as she is, who rules the lower floor of the yetis' cave. In the base game he drops a Compass and a pile of rope. In Shell Game the Compass is replaced by this check's reward, and the Compass is shuffled to a check somewhere in the run.

How to reach it

Go through the yetis' cave doorway, as for the Queen, and down past her floor. The King paces the floor below hers, with a pit of spikes beneath him and the cave's way out at the bottom.

How to do it

He has 10 health and can't be stunned. Get in front of him, or close to him on his floor, and he roars: the screen shakes, ice falls from the ceiling in front of him and can stun you, and his icy breath freezes whatever it touches. The ice doesn't fall above or behind him.

  • Stay above him. If you're wearing Spike Shoes, jumping on him hurts him; without them, he punches up at you and can leave you in the path of his breath.
  • Bombs or a mine work well. Bomb the floor under him and he drops onto the spikes below.
  • A Shotgun or other ranged weapon helps, but watch for falling ice while you use it.

Pick up the reward where he died.

Mothership

Check · 5-1, back layer · needs the Alien Compass

What it is

An alien ship hidden in the Abyss at the bottom of the Ice Caves, five floors tall, defended by force fields and by machines that keep making copies of shopkeepers. Partway up, behind four force fields, the base game keeps a Plasma Cannon. In Shell Game that spot holds this check's reward, and the Plasma Cannon is shuffled to a check somewhere in the run.

How to reach it

You need the Alien Compass; Shell Game always makes it available before the Mothership. In the base game it's Van Horsing's gift in the Temple, the Van Horsing's reward check.

  1. Head down past the exit to the bottom of the level, where the camera stops following you down.
  2. The entrance is a single-tile ledge just below that point, over the Abyss. Its place changes from run to run, and the Alien Compass's arrow points to it. Line yourself up above it before you drop, because missing it means falling into the Abyss.
  3. Go through the door on the ledge into the Mothership.

How to do it

  1. The bottom floor is the entrance, crossed with force fields.
  2. The second floor holds Lahamu, who is a check of her own. Killing her switches off every force field in the ship for good.
  3. The reward is on the third floor, above her, behind four force fields. With Lahamu dead, walk in and take it. Without killing her, an explosion close to the force fields can get you through.

The shopkeeper copies aren't real shopkeepers: killing them doesn't turn the shops of the run against you. The floors above the reward hold more of the machines and nothing you need.

Lahamu

Check · boss of the Mothership · needs the Alien Compass

What it is

The alien mind at the heart of the Mothership. She never moves; she defends herself with psychic blasts, force fields and the machines beside her. In the base game she leaves only a few gems. In Shell Game, she also leaves this check's reward where she dies.

How to reach it

Get into the Mothership as its entry describes, and climb to the second floor. She sits in the middle of it, with a force field and a shopkeeper-making machine on either side, and a coffin on one side.

How to do it

She has 20 health. Come close and a targeting ring appears over you. After a moment it stops following you, then blasts everything inside it, for 10 damage and a stun. Keep moving: once the ring locks in place, get out of it.

  • Come at her from the coffin's side. The machine on the other side can make a shopkeeper copy carrying a Shotgun.
  • There's a one-block gap between her and the ceiling. With Spike Shoes, jump on her from there, about four times.
  • A Teleporter or Telepack that lands you inside her kills her outright.
  • If you're using a Shotgun or Plasma Cannon, break the force fields beside her first, or your shots bounce off them back at you.

A blast she has already aimed still goes off after she dies, so stay out of the ring before you pick up the reward. Her death switches off the ship's force fields, which opens the way to the Mothership's own reward on the floor above.
