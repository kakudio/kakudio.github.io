Ice Caves

A guide to the Ice Caves, for players of Shell Game

The Ice Caves

The Ice Caves are a single, very tall level, 5-1. Every run comes through them, whichever way it went after Olmec: from the Tide Pool or the Temple, and from their hidden areas too. The level leads on to Neo Babylon.

The level comes in three parts. You start at the top, among floating platforms of ice. Partway down, a doorway leads into the yetis' cave, where the Yeti Queen and Yeti King live. Below that is the stretch with the exit, and under it the Abyss, with the entrance to the Mothership hidden at its very bottom.

The Yeti Queen and King

Partway down the level, a doorway leads into the yetis' cave. Two huge yetis rule it: the Yeti Queen paces the upper floor with a crowd of ordinary yetis around her, and her partner the Yeti King, as big as she is, the floor below hers. Under the King is a pit of spikes, with the cave's way out at the bottom.

The cave is optional: with a Cape, Climbing Gloves or a Jetpack you can drop past it to the exit, so come in on purpose. Once inside you can leave the way you came in after the Queen if you'd rather not take on the King.

They have a lot in common. Each has 10 health and can't be stunned, and each drops a pile of rope when they die: the Queen with a pair of Spike Shoes, the King with a Compass. Jumping on either does nothing unless you're already wearing Spike Shoes; without them, stand on a yeti's head and they punch up at you. Where they differ is how they come at you.

The Queen leaps. When she spots you, she lets out a yell and jumps at you, landing with a slam that throws you, items and nearby yetis around. Stand on her head and she punches up, then throws herself at you while you're knocked about.

The King roars. Get in front of him, or close to him on his floor, and the screen shakes, ice falls from the ceiling in front of him and can stun you, and his icy breath freezes whatever it touches. The ice doesn't fall above or behind him, so stay above him; a punch up at you there can leave you in the path of his breath.

Ways to deal with them:

  • Keep your distance and hurt them from range: bombs, a mine, a Shotgun, or the bodies of the yetis around them, thrown at them. Against the King, watch for falling ice while you shoot.
  • Bomb the floor out from under them. The Queen falls to the King's floor, where his breath may freeze her, and bombing the floor under the King, or under both of them, drops them onto the spikes at the bottom of the cave.

Pick up what each one leaves where they died.

The Mothership and Lahamu

An alien ship is hidden in the Abyss at the bottom of the Ice Caves, five floors tall, defended by force fields and by machines that keep making copies of shopkeepers. Lahamu, the alien mind at its heart, sits on the second floor, and partway up, behind four force fields, is a Plasma Cannon.

Finding the way in takes the Alien Compass, which in the base game is Van Horsing's gift in the Temple.

In Shell Game The Alien Compass is always somewhere you can get it before the Mothership.

  1. Head down past the exit to the bottom of the level, where the camera stops following you down.
  2. The entrance is a single-tile ledge just below that point, over the Abyss. Its place changes from run to run, and the Alien Compass's arrow points to it. Line yourself up above it before you drop, because missing it means falling into the Abyss.
  3. Go through the door on the ledge into the Mothership.

Inside, the bottom floor is the entrance, crossed with force fields. Lahamu is on the second floor, and killing her switches off every force field in the ship for good. The Plasma Cannon is on the third floor, above her, behind its four force fields: with Lahamu dead, walk in and take it. Without killing her, an explosion close to the force fields can get you through. The floors above hold more of the machines and nothing you need.

The shopkeeper copies aren't real shopkeepers: killing them doesn't turn the shops of the run against you.

Lahamu

Lahamu never moves; she defends herself with psychic blasts, force fields and the machines beside her. She sits in the middle of the second floor, with a force field and a shopkeeper-making machine on either side, and a coffin on one side.

She has 20 health. Come close and a targeting ring appears over you. After a moment it stops following you, then blasts everything inside it, for 10 damage and a stun. Keep moving: once the ring locks in place, get out of it.

  • Come at her from the coffin's side. The machine on the other side can make a shopkeeper copy carrying a Shotgun.
  • There's a one-block gap between her and the ceiling. With Spike Shoes, jump on her from there, about four times.
  • A Teleporter or Telepack that lands you inside her kills her outright.
  • If you're using a Shotgun or Plasma Cannon, break the force fields beside her first, or your shots bounce off them back at you.

A blast she has already aimed still goes off after she dies, so stay out of the ring before you go near where she fell. In the base game she leaves only a few gems.

In Shell Game Lahamu also leaves an item from your run where she dies.
