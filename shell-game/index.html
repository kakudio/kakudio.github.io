How Shell Game works

The ultimate goal of the game remains the same: beat Hundun with the Arrow of Light and Hou Yi's Bow, and access the Cosmic Ocean. However, the items you need to get there have moved. Any boss or quest in the game might have an item you want or need to finish the run.

Each seed is replayable, letting you build a new route one run at a time. Every item, from the Ankh down to a pair of Spring Shoes, turns up in the same place until you change seeds, while the game's regular generation logic continues to provide the variety you are accustomed to. You can learn a specific seed and move on when you want a new challenge.

Spelunky 2 has locations and quests that always provide the same items: Olmec's Ankh, Vlad's Crown, Excalibur in its stone, the Tablet of Destiny from Kingu or Osiris, the Bow and the Arrow of Light from the Moon and Sun Challenges. Shell Game takes the important moments (bosses, quests, etc.), called checks, and assigns each one a reward from the game's item pool.

The following items are considered progression items and will always be available in the route before they are needed in game.

  • The Udjat Eye is available before the Black Market and Vlad's Castle.
  • The Skeleton Key is available before the locked room at Madame Tusk's Dice House.
  • Excalibur is available before the Kingu fight.
  • The Scepter is available before the City of Gold.
  • The Ankh, the Crown and the Hedjet are available before the Kingu and Osiris boss fights.
  • The Alien Compass is available before the Mothership and the Lahamu fight.
  • The Eggplant is available by the Ice Caves.
  • The Tablet of Destiny and Madame Tusk's Idol are available by Neo Babylon.
  • Hou Yi's Bow and the Arrow of Light are available before Hundun.

Every seed has a way to the Cosmic Ocean. Before your run starts, the mod builds the whole layout from the seed and checks it: at least one way through the game has to collect the Tablet of Destiny, Hou Yi's Bow and the Arrow of Light and reach Hundun, and every check has to be reachable on some way through.

Options

You'll find them in Modlunky 2's options menu, or in Overlunky under F8 → Options.

Enable Shell Game
Turns the whole mod on or off.
Randomizer Seed and New Seed
Randomizer Seed fixes your layout. Set a number to play that layout every time, or leave it at 0 and your next run picks a new seed, which stays set for the runs after it. New Seed sets it back to 0, so your next run gets a new layout.
Kali Item Recovery
Brings the items you were holding and wearing in the City of Gold, and the altar drops it allows, through to the Duat.
Balanced Duat Kali Rewards

Changes what Kali's altar in the Duat gives you for your favor. The more favor you've built up with Kali, the bigger the reward:

  • a Player Bag of 5 bombs and 5 ropes, instead of a cooked turkey;
  • a Player Bag of 10 bombs and 10 ropes, instead of a Bomb Bag;
  • at the top, a Player Bag of 10 bombs and 10 ropes with Royal Jelly, instead of a Bomb Box. You get one Royal Jelly, and another for each further step of favor, so a run that keeps Kali happy keeps getting more.
True Crown Restoration
After the True Crown encounter, Kali's curse is lifted and you are restored to 4 health.

Install

You need:

  • Spelunky 2 on Steam;
  • Modlunky 2, from its releases on GitHub;
  • Playlunky, which you install from the Playlunky tab in Modlunky 2.

Then:

  1. Download Shell Game's zip file from the latest release.
  2. Open Modlunky 2.
  3. In the Mods tab, choose Open Mods Folder.
  4. Extract the zip into that folder.
  5. In Modlunky 2's Playlunky tab, find Shell Game in the list of mods and turn it on.
  6. Press Play! to start Spelunky 2 with Shell Game.

These steps follow the Installation section of Shell Game's README. If the two ever disagree, go with the README.

What's still rough in the beta

I've seen every one of the 40 checks give up its item in-game. These are the parts I haven't confirmed yet:

  • Multiplayer. I haven't checked which player gets credit when something happens in a co-op run. For now, Kali's first gift goes to the first player in the game, whoever made the offering.
  • True Crown Restoration. I haven't yet seen it lift the curse and restore your health in a real run, or confirmed it leaves your health alone when you aren't cursed.

If you run into either, or anything else, I want to hear about it.

Found a bug?

At the start of every run, Shell Game writes a run log to Mods\Data\ShellGame\run_reports in your Spelunky 2 folder. It holds your seeds, the full layout for the run and the mod's logs, which is what I need to see what went wrong. It keeps your newest 30 runs.

Keep Generate Spoiler and Generate Logs on if your build shows them in the options, so the run log has both. Builds that don't show them always write both.

The run log gives away the whole layout, so don't open it while you're still playing that seed.

Report a bug

On the bug-report page you pick the run log for the run that went wrong, tell me what happened, and send it. No account needed.
