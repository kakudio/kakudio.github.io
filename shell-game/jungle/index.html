Jungle

A guide to the Jungle, for players of Shell Game

The Jungle

The Jungle is levels 2-1 to 2-4. It is one of the two ways from the Dwelling to Olmec; the other is Volcana. You take the Jungle by leaving 1-4 through its left exit, and a run that does never sees Volcana.

Its last three levels, 2-2 to 2-4, are where most of it happens: a trapped sister on each of them, and on one of them Tun's Moon Challenge, on another the hidden Black Market. Some Jungle levels also have a beehive, with the Queen Bee inside.

The Three Sisters

Three sisters, Parsley, Parsnip and Parmesan, are trapped in the Jungle, one on each of its last three levels: Parsley on 2-2, Parsnip on 2-3 and Parmesan on 2-4. Each is in a small stone room in that level's back layer, which has a single way in from the front. The ones you free wait for you in Olmec's Lair with a thank-you gift that grows with the number you saved: a Rope Pile for one, a Bomb Bag for two and a Bomb Box for all three.

  1. On each of the three levels, find the door to the back layer and the sister's stone room.
  2. Push the stone block out of the way and walk up to her. She's rescued from then on, and you can carry on with the level.
  3. Keep her safe while you're there. Angering or killing any of the sisters ends the quest: there will be no gift at Olmec, however many you save afterwards.
  4. In Olmec's Lair, climb the ladder at the far right of the top floor to the sisters' ledge, and pick up the gift.

In Shell Game Only the gift for all three sisters is a check: save every one and it's an item from your run instead of the Bomb Box. Saving one or two still gets you the base game's Rope Pile or Bomb Bag, but not the check.

The beehive and the Queen Bee

A beehive appears only on some Jungle levels, which tell you so with a message about buzzing as the level starts, so not every run has one. The hive is a block of honeycomb rooms full of bees, and in the middle of it lives the Queen Bee, a giant bee with a little crown. When she dies she drops Royal Jelly, which restores 6 health.

  1. Clear or dodge the ordinary bees as you work your way into the hive.
  2. Fight the Queen Bee. She flies erratically, does 2 damage when she touches you, and can't be hurt by jumping on her unless you wear Spike Shoes. She has 10 health.
  3. Hit her with your whip between her swoops, or from somewhere she's too big to follow you into. A ranged weapon from a safe spot works too, and a single Paste bomb that sticks to her does the job in one go.
  4. Pick up her drop where she falls.

In Shell Game Because a run can have no beehive at all, the Queen Bee only ever drops gear from the treasure chest on How Shell Game works, never a progression item a run needs.

Moon Challenge

One of Tun's paid challenges: a dark room packed with earth, where you dig with a Mattock for Hou Yi's Bow and the Metal Arrow that comes with it, sealed in a pocket of brick. Tun stands outside the challenge's door on one of 2-2, 2-3 or 2-4. Talk to her and pay the entry fee, $10,000 in the base game, and the way in opens.

Volcana has its own Moon Challenge, but a run only ever passes through one of the two areas.

  1. Pick up the Mattock Tun gives you. It breaks after a limited number of swings, so dig with purpose rather than clearing the whole room.
  2. Dig down. The brick pocket is in the lower part of the room. Watch for scorpions and arrow traps in the dark.
  3. Break into the pocket and take the prize.

Don't break the platform or the force fields at the entrance, and don't dig into the challenge from above: either one angers Tun.

In Shell Game The brick pocket holds an item from your run, and the Metal Arrow that comes with the Bow is gone too. The Jungle's and Volcana's Moon Challenges are separate checks.

Black Market

A hidden street of shops in the Jungle's back layer, selling rarer things than an ordinary shop. Its priciest item is the Hedjet, at $40,000 in the base game.

The market is on one of 2-2, 2-3 or 2-4, never on the same level as the Moon Challenge, and you need the Udjat Eye to find it. On that level the Eye starts blinking as you get close to the entrance, and shows you a doorway hidden inside the rock. Break through to the doorway with a bomb or a Mattock and go in.

  1. Bring money: collect gold on the way through the Dwelling and the Jungle.
  2. Find the most expensive item in the market.
  3. Pick it up and buy it as you would in any shop.

You can also take it without paying. Every shopkeeper in the market then turns on you, and they stay angry for the rest of the run.

In Shell Game The Udjat Eye is always somewhere you can get it before the Black Market. The Hedjet's spot sells an item from your run instead, still at the Hedjet's price, and the rest of the market sells what it always does.
