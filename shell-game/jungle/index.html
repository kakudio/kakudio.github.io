Jungle

A Shell Game guide to the checks in the Jungle

The Jungle's checks

The Jungle is levels 2-1 to 2-4. It is one of the two ways from the Dwelling to Olmec; the other is Volcana. You take the Jungle by leaving 1-4 through its left exit, and a run that does never sees Volcana, so its checks wait for another run.

In Shell Game each check here holds whatever your seed put there, not the item the base game gives.

  • Black Market
  • Queen Bee
  • Moon Challenge
  • The Three Sisters

Black Market

Check · 2-2, 2-3 or 2-4, back layer · needs the Udjat Eye

What it is

A hidden street of shops in the Jungle's back layer, selling rarer things than an ordinary shop. Its priciest item in the base game is the Hedjet. In Shell Game that spot sells this check's reward instead, at the price the Hedjet would have cost, and the Hedjet is shuffled to a check somewhere in the run. The rest of the market sells what it always does.

How to reach it

You need the Udjat Eye; Shell Game always makes it available before the Black Market. The market is on one of 2-2, 2-3 or 2-4, never on the same level as the Moon Challenge. On that level the Eye starts blinking as you get close to the entrance, and shows you a doorway hidden inside the rock. Break through to the doorway with a bomb or a Mattock and go in.

How to do it

  1. Bring money. The Hedjet's spot costs $40,000 in the base game, so collect gold on the way through the Dwelling and the Jungle.
  2. Find the most expensive item in the market. That's the check.
  3. Pick it up and buy it as you would in any shop.

You can also take it without paying. Every shopkeeper in the market then turns on you, and they stay angry for the rest of the run.

Queen Bee

Check · a Jungle level with a beehive

What it is

A giant bee with a little crown who lives in the middle of a beehive. In the base game she drops Royal Jelly, which restores 6 health. In Shell Game she drops this check's reward instead.

Not every run has a beehive, so the Queen Bee can be missed through no fault of yours. That's why Shell Game only ever gives her gear from the treasure chest on How Shell Game works, never a progression item a run needs.

How to reach it

A beehive appears only on some Jungle levels, which tell you so with a message about buzzing as the level starts. The hive is a block of honeycomb rooms full of bees, and the Queen Bee is inside it.

How to do it

  1. Clear or dodge the ordinary bees as you work your way into the hive.
  2. Fight the Queen Bee. She flies erratically, does 2 damage when she touches you, and can't be hurt by jumping on her unless you wear Spike Shoes. She has 10 health.
  3. Hit her with your whip between her swoops, or from somewhere she's too big to follow you into. A ranged weapon from a safe spot works too, and a single Paste bomb that sticks to her does the job in one go.
  4. Pick up the reward where she falls.

Moon Challenge

Check · 2-2, 2-3 or 2-4 · also in Volcana

What it is

One of Tun's paid challenges: a dark room packed with earth, where you dig for a prize with a Mattock. In the base game the prize is Hou Yi's Bow, sealed in a pocket of brick. In Shell Game the Bow is shuffled to a check somewhere in the run, and the pocket holds this check's reward. The Metal Arrow that comes with the Bow is gone too.

The Moon Challenge comes in a Jungle version and a Volcana version. They are separate checks, but a run only ever passes through one of the two areas.

How to reach it

Tun stands outside the challenge's door on one of 2-2, 2-3 or 2-4. Talk to her and pay the entry fee, $10,000 in the base game, and the way in opens.

How to do it

  1. Pick up the Mattock Tun gives you. It breaks after a limited number of swings, so dig with purpose rather than clearing the whole room.
  2. Dig down. The reward's brick pocket is in the lower part of the room. Watch for scorpions and arrow traps in the dark.
  3. Break into the pocket and take the reward.

Don't break the platform or the force fields at the entrance, and don't dig into the challenge from above: either one angers Tun.

The Three Sisters

Check · rescued on 2-2, 2-3 and 2-4 · collected at Olmec

What it is

Three sisters, Parsley, Parsnip and Parmesan, are trapped in the Jungle, one on each of its last three levels. The ones you free wait for you in Olmec's Lair with a thank-you gift. In the base game the gift grows with the number you saved: a Rope Pile for one, a Bomb Bag for two and a Bomb Box for all three.

In Shell Game the check is the gift for all three: save every sister and it's this check's reward instead of the Bomb Box. Saving one or two still gets you the base game's Rope Pile or Bomb Bag, but not the check.

How to reach it

Parsley is on 2-2, Parsnip on 2-3 and Parmesan on 2-4. Each is in a small stone room in that level's back layer, which has a single way in from the front.

How to do it

  1. On each of the three levels, find the door to the back layer and the sister's stone room.
  2. Push the stone block out of the way and walk up to her. She's rescued from then on, and you can carry on with the level.
  3. Keep her safe while you're there. Angering or killing any of the sisters ends the quest: there will be no gift at Olmec, however many you save afterwards.
  4. In Olmec's Lair, climb the ladder at the far right of the top floor to the sisters' ledge, and pick up the reward.
