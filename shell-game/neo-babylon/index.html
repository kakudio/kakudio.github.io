Neo Babylon

A guide to Neo Babylon, for players of Shell Game

Neo Babylon

Neo Babylon is levels 6-1 to 6-3, a city of the Olmites full of force fields, laser traps, crushing elevators and spark traps, with UFOs overhead. Every run reaches it from the Ice Caves, and it leads on to Tiamat's Throne at 6-4.

6-2 holds a hall of Ushabti. The one the Tablet of Destiny marks, carried to 6-3, hatches into the Qilin, the easiest ride past Tiamat to the Sunken City. A run to the Cosmic Ocean usually wants it.

6-3 is where two stories from earlier in the run end, at Madame Tusk's Palace of Pleasure and the vault beneath it. The palace only appears if Madame Tusk is still alive, so a run that killed her in the Tide Pool loses both.

Madame Tusk's Palace and Tusk's Vault

Madame Tusk entertains her favourite guests in her Palace of Pleasure on 6-3, under the eye of eight bodyguards. A guest she invited may help themselves to the luxuries on its two floors, among them a Royal Jelly. Beneath it is her vault, which Sparrow the thief has spent the run planning to rob. One visit to 6-3 can take in both, in that order.

The palace needs her invitation, which you earn at her Dice House in the Tide Pool by winning all five of its prizes (see Madame Tusk's Dice House). In the base game winning $50,000 in all also gets you invited.

In Shell Game The palace holds an item from your run where the Royal Jelly would be, and only a run through the Tide Pool can earn the invitation to it: a run through the Temple never reaches the Dice House.

Don't kill Madame Tusk in the Tide Pool, or anger her there: if she's dead the palace doesn't appear, and if she's angry but alive her bodyguards on 6-3 shoot you on sight.

  1. On 6-3, find the palace and go in. Madame Tusk waits at the top and greets you as a VIP.
  2. Walk the palace's two floors and pick up the Royal Jelly. As an invited guest you can take it, and the other luxuries, without anyone minding.
  3. The basement below holds a Kali altar and, behind a force field, the level's cursed pot.

If you mean to break into the vault as well, take what you want from the palace first. Breaking the vault's force fields turns Madame Tusk and every bodyguard against you.

Tusk's Vault and the end of Sparrow's quest

The vault under the palace holds eight chests of large gems behind three force fields, a laser trap, a locked door and two bodyguards. If you've helped Sparrow through the run, she's waiting at its door and offers to split the treasure with you. Take your half, and no more, and she rewards you with a bag of 24 ropes.

She only waits at the vault if you've kept her quest going. The whole quest is on the Sparrow entry; in short:

  1. Meet her. Steal from a shop before Olmec's Lair, so the shopkeeper shouts "Stop, thief!", and she turns up a level or two later in a back-layer hideout. Talk to her there (the Shoplifter check); leaving the level without doing so ends her quest.
  2. Steal Madame Tusk's Idol. In the base game it's in the locked room above her Dice House in the Tide Pool (the Idol Heist), and you carry it out through the level's exit. If she or her bodyguard catch you with it they attack, but the quest goes on.
  3. Find her on 6-1. She's hiding in the back layer of the first level of Neo Babylon. Talk to her; leaving 6-1 without doing so ends her quest.

In Shell Game Madame Tusk's Idol is shuffled: carry it through a level exit, wherever you found it, to move her quest on. It turns up no earlier than the Tide Pool and no later than Neo Babylon, and that includes the palace and the vault on 6-3. Those come after Sparrow's meeting on 6-1, so if the Idol is there, her quest may not reach the vault.

Killing Madame Tusk before the Idol is stolen, or provoking Sparrow at any point, also ends the quest: she fights back with a crossbow.

With all that done, Sparrow has dealt with the two bodyguards at the vault and unlocked its door. On 6-3, make your way down through the palace to the vault door, near the bottom of the level, where she's waiting.

  1. Talk to Sparrow. She offers to split the treasure if you can get past the force fields.
  2. Get through the three force fields. A bomb against each one is the simplest way. Breaking them angers Madame Tusk and all her bodyguards, so finish with the palace before you start. A Teleporter that carries you across the force fields instead of breaking them keeps her calm.
  3. Mind the laser trap on the way in.
  4. Open exactly four of the eight chests, and keep their gems. Your half is four: open a fifth and Sparrow turns on you, and her quest is over.
  5. Go back and talk to Sparrow. She leaves the ropes on the floor beside her.

You can break into the vault without Sparrow and take all eight chests, but then there's no one to give you her reward.
