A Shell Game guide to the checks in Neo Babylon

Neo Babylon's checks

Neo Babylon is levels 6-1 to 6-3, a city of the Olmites full of force fields, laser traps, crushing elevators and spark traps, with UFOs overhead. Every run reaches it from the Ice Caves, and it leads on to Tiamat's Throne at 6-4.

Both its checks are on 6-3, at Madame Tusk's Palace of Pleasure, and both are the end of a story that starts earlier in the run. The palace only appears if Madame Tusk is still alive, so a run that killed her in the Tide Pool loses both checks.

6-2 holds a hall of Ushabti. The one the Tablet of Destiny marks, carried to 6-3, hatches into the Qilin, the easiest ride past Tiamat to the Sunken City. It isn't a check, but a run to the Cosmic Ocean usually wants it.

In Shell Game each check here holds whatever your seed put there, not the item the base game gives.

Madame Tusk's Palace

Check · 6-3, back layer · needs Madame Tusk's invitation

What it is

Madame Tusk's Palace of Pleasure, where she entertains her favourite guests under the eye of eight bodyguards. A guest she invited may help themselves to the luxuries on its two floors, among them a Royal Jelly. In Shell Game the Royal Jelly is replaced by this check's reward, and Royal Jelly is shuffled to a check somewhere in the run.

How to reach it

You need her invitation, which you earn at her Dice House in the Tide Pool: win all five prizes there, the Madame Tusk's Dice House check. In the base game winning $50,000 in all also gets you invited. A run through the Temple never reaches the Dice House, so it can't earn the invitation.

Don't kill Madame Tusk in the Tide Pool, or anger her there: if she's dead the palace doesn't appear, and if she's angry but alive her bodyguards on 6-3 shoot you on sight.

On 6-3, find the palace and go in. Madame Tusk waits at the top and greets you as a VIP.

How to do it

  1. Walk the palace's two floors and pick up the reward where the Royal Jelly would be. As an invited guest you can take it, and the other luxuries, without anyone minding.
  2. The basement below holds a Kali altar and, behind a force field, the level's cursed pot.

If you mean to break into Tusk's Vault as well, take this reward first. Breaking the vault's force fields turns Madame Tusk and every bodyguard against you.

Tusk's Vault

Check · 6-3, back layer · the end of Sparrow's quest

What it is

The vault under Madame Tusk's palace: eight chests of large gems behind three force fields, a laser trap, a locked door and two bodyguards. Sparrow, a thief, has spent the run working towards robbing it, and if you've helped her she offers to split it with you. Take your half, and no more, and she rewards you. In the base game the reward is a bag of 24 ropes; in Shell Game it's this check's reward, and the Bag of ropes is shuffled to a check somewhere in the run.

How to reach it

Sparrow only waits at the vault if you've kept her quest going through the run. The whole quest is on the Sparrow entry; in short:

  1. Meet her. Steal from a shop before Olmec's Lair, so the shopkeeper shouts "Stop, thief!", and she turns up a level or two later in a back-layer hideout. Talk to her there; leaving the level without doing so ends her quest. That's the Shoplifter check.
  2. Steal Madame Tusk's Idol. In the base game it's in the locked room above her Dice House in the Tide Pool, and you carry it out through the level's exit. If she or her bodyguard catch you with it they attack, but the quest goes on. In Shell Game that room holds the Idol Heist check's reward instead, and Madame Tusk's Idol is shuffled: carry it through a level exit, wherever you found it, to move her quest on. Shell Game places it no earlier than the Tide Pool and no later than Neo Babylon, and that includes Madame Tusk's Palace and Tusk's Vault on 6-3. Those come after Sparrow's meeting on 6-1, so on a seed that puts the Idol there, her quest may not reach the vault.
  3. Find her on 6-1. She's hiding in the back layer of the first level of Neo Babylon. Talk to her; leaving 6-1 without doing so ends her quest.

Killing Madame Tusk before the Idol is stolen, or provoking Sparrow at any point, also ends the quest: she fights back with a crossbow.

With all that done, Sparrow has dealt with the two bodyguards at the vault and unlocked its door. On 6-3, make your way down through the palace to the vault door, near the bottom of the level, where she's waiting.

How to do it

  1. Talk to Sparrow. She offers to split the treasure if you can get past the force fields.
  2. Get through the three force fields. A bomb against each one is the simplest way. Breaking them angers Madame Tusk and all her bodyguards, so take the palace's reward before you start. A Teleporter that carries you across the force fields instead of breaking them keeps her calm.
  3. Mind the laser trap on the way in.
  4. Open exactly four of the eight chests, and keep their gems. Your half is four: open a fifth and Sparrow turns on you, and her quest is over.
  5. Go back and talk to Sparrow. She leaves the reward on the floor beside her.

You can break into the vault without Sparrow and take all eight chests, but then there's no one to give you this check's reward.
