Olmec

A guide to Olmec's Lair, for players of Shell Game

Olmec's Lair

Olmec's Lair is level 3-1, a single level you reach from either the Jungle or Volcana. It is three floors of brick stacked over a pool of lava, with a giant stone head on the loose in the middle. Beating him is also the way to the Ankh, hidden in the back layer behind the lair.

If you came through the Jungle and saved all Three Sisters, they're waiting on a ledge up the ladder at the far right of the top floor, with their gift.

The level has two ways on. The way to the Tide Pool is at the right of the middle floor, marked by three crabs; the way to the Temple is at the far right of the bottom floor, marked by three locusts.

Olmec, and the Ankh behind him

Olmec is a huge stone head that crushes its way down through the floors of its lair. You can't hurt it: bombs and Mattocks do nothing, and a Plasma Cannon's shots bounce back at you. Instead you beat Olmec by tricking him into smashing through every floor until he sinks into the lava at the bottom.

He starts in the middle of the top floor as soon as you arrive, and the fight changes each time he breaks through to a new floor.

  1. Top floor. Olmec jumps toward you. If he lands above you, he pauses and then drops straight down, crushing anything underneath and breaking a row of floor. Stand where you want a hole, wait for him to come over, and move out from under him before he drops. Landings that miss you break nothing.
  2. Middle floor. He floats on two small hovering platforms and lobs bombs out of both sides of his face while you're below his eyes, three rounds of them before he stops. Break both platforms, which take a single hit each, and he crashes down through two rows of floor before rising again on new ones.
  3. Bottom floor. He jumps and slams as on the top floor, and when a slam breaks the floor he lets a UFO out of each side of his mouth, again every couple of slams after that. Keep moving, deal with the UFOs, and keep luring him down.
  4. Once he's through the last floor he sinks into the lava, and the fight is over.

Riding him down to the Ankh

The Ankh waits at the top of the back layer behind Olmec's Lair: carry it and it brings you back to life once when you die. The way in is a row of doors into the back layer just above the lava, with nothing to stand on beside them. The only footing is Olmec's own head as he sinks.

  1. As Olmec sinks into the lava, drop onto the top of his head.
  2. Go through the nearest back-layer door while you're level with it. The doors are spaced so one is always within reach, even with Olmec sitting between two of them, so you don't need to line him up.
  3. Climb to the top of the back layer and take the Ankh.
