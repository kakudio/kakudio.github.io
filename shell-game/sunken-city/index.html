Sunken City

A guide to the Sunken City, for players of Shell Game

The Sunken City

The Sunken City is levels 7-1 to 7-3, the hidden world above Tiamat's Throne. You only get here from Tiamat's level, by climbing up past her once she's beaten; the Tiamat guide covers the way up. After 7-3 comes Hundun's Hideaway, the last stop before the Cosmic Ocean.

On 7-1, a statue beside a sealed door is the way into Eggplant World, which takes the place of 7-2 for a run that opens it. Somewhere in these three levels Tun also runs her last and hardest challenge, the Sun Challenge.

Sun Challenge

The last of Tun's paid challenges, and the hardest: a sealed arena where you have to stay alive while monsters pour in. The prize is the Arrow of Light, the arrow that opens the way to the Cosmic Ocean at Hundun.

Tun waits beside the challenge's door in a corner of one of 7-1, 7-2 or 7-3; it is most often on 7-1. Talk to her and pay the entry fee, $50,000 in the base game, and the way in opens. Save your gold through the earlier worlds if you mean to take it on.

  1. Go in prepared: full health if you can manage it, bombs, and a weapon you can keep using, such as a Shotgun, a Freeze Ray or a Web Gun.
  2. Once you're in, the countdown starts and tough monsters keep dropping in from spawners in the ceiling: vampires, sorceresses, necromancers and the like. Survive for 30 seconds.
  3. Find a corner you can defend rather than running around the room. Blowing up the spawner above a corner and holding that corner leaves you with far less to fight.
  4. When the time runs out the monsters stop coming. Take the Arrow of Light from the prize spot.

Winning pays twice. On top of the Arrow, the game gives you a stitched bag of bombs and ropes, 24 of each, to see you through what's left of the Sunken City. It appears in the arena once you've won; pick it up along with the Arrow before you leave.

Dying in the arena fails the challenge, even if an Ankh brings you back, and you don't get another try. Attacking Tun or breaking into the arena some other way angers her.

In Shell Game The prize spot and the supplies are two separate checks, and the supplies only turn up once you win, so both cost the same fee and the same 30 seconds. The Arrow of Light itself is shuffled like any other item, but it is always somewhere you can get it before Hundun.
