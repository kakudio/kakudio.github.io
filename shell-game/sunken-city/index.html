A Shell Game guide to the checks in the Sunken City

The Sunken City's checks

The Sunken City is levels 7-1 to 7-3, the hidden world above Tiamat's Throne. You only get here from Tiamat's level, by climbing up past her once she's beaten; the Tiamat guide covers the way up. After 7-3 comes Hundun's Hideaway, the last stop before the Cosmic Ocean.

On 7-1, a statue beside a sealed door is the way into Eggplant World, which takes the place of 7-2 for a run that opens it.

Both checks here come from the same challenge. In Shell Game each holds whatever your seed put there, not the item the base game gives.

Sun Challenge

Check · 7-1, 7-2 or 7-3

What it is

The last of Tun's paid challenges, and the hardest: a sealed arena where you have to stay alive while monsters pour in. In the base game the prize is the Arrow of Light, the arrow that opens the way to the Cosmic Ocean. In Shell Game the prize spot holds this check's reward, and the Arrow of Light is shuffled to a check somewhere in the run; Shell Game always makes it available before Hundun.

Winning also earns a second, separate reward: see Sun Challenge supplies.

How to reach it

Tun waits beside the challenge's door in a corner of one of 7-1, 7-2 or 7-3; it is most often on 7-1. Talk to her and pay the entry fee, $50,000 in the base game, and the way in opens. Save your gold through the earlier worlds if you mean to take it on.

How to do it

  1. Go in prepared: full health if you can manage it, bombs, and a weapon you can keep using, such as a Shotgun, a Freeze Ray or a Web Gun.
  2. Once you're in, the countdown starts and tough monsters keep dropping in from spawners in the ceiling: vampires, sorceresses, necromancers and the like. Survive for 30 seconds.
  3. Find a corner you can defend rather than running around the room. Blowing up the spawner above a corner and holding that corner leaves you with far less to fight.
  4. When the time runs out the monsters stop coming. Take the reward from the prize spot.

Dying in the arena fails the challenge, even if an Ankh brings you back, and you don't get another try. Attacking Tun or breaking into the arena some other way angers her.

Sun Challenge supplies

Check · 7-1, 7-2 or 7-3 · after the Sun Challenge

What it is

When you survive the Sun Challenge, the base game gives you a stitched bag holding 24 bombs and 24 ropes on top of the Arrow of Light, to see you through what's left of the Sunken City. Shell Game counts that bag as a check of its own: the bag is replaced by this check's reward, and the bag of bombs and ropes is shuffled to a check somewhere in the run.

How to reach it

It only turns up when you win the Sun Challenge, so it costs the same entry fee and takes you through the same arena.

How to do it

  1. Survive the Sun Challenge to the end of its countdown.
  2. The reward appears in the arena once you've won. Pick it up along with the Sun Challenge's own prize before you leave.
