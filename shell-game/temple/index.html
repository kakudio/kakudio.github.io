A Shell Game guide to the checks in the Temple of Anubis, the City of Gold and the Duat

The Temple's checks

The Temple of Anubis is levels 4-1 to 4-4: sandstone halls full of mummies, crush traps and crocodile men. It is one of the two ways on from Olmec's Lair; the other is the Tide Pool. You take the Temple through the door at the far right of Olmec's bottom floor, the one marked by locusts, and a run that does never sees the Tide Pool, so its checks wait for another run.

Anubis and Van Horsing are on 4-1 and the Stars Challenge is on 4-2. The last two checks are hidden behind two secret levels. The Scepter opens a golden door on 4-2 into the City of Gold, which takes the place of 4-3; dying there with the Ankh takes you to the Duat, which takes the place of 4-4 and is where Anubis II and Osiris wait. Either way the area ends at the Ice Caves.

In Shell Game each check here holds whatever your seed put there, not the item the base game gives.

Anubis

Check · boss of 4-1

What it is

The jackal-headed god of the Temple, asleep on his throne. In the base game he drops his Scepter when he dies, the key, with a crown, to the City of Gold. In Shell Game he drops this check's reward instead, and the Scepter is shuffled to a check somewhere in the run. Shell Game never puts the Scepter in the Tide Pool, and always makes it available before the City of Gold.

How to reach it

Every 4-1 has him, sitting on his throne on the main path through the level.

How to do it

He's far easier asleep than awake. Getting close to him, setting off an explosion nearby or hitting him wakes him: he blinks three times, then rises. Even asleep, touching him hurts.

  • Before he wakes. With Spike Shoes, jump on him while he sleeps. With Paste on your bombs, two stuck to him finish him, awake or not. Either way his 20 health goes quickly.
  • Once he's awake. He floats about, keeps out of reach of your whip, and fires slow psychic blasts from his Scepter that pass through walls and take 10 health, enough to kill you outright. After every three, an eye flashes where you're standing and a faster, bigger blast follows it there: move off that spot. Fight him with bombs and anything you can throw or shoot, from a distance, and keep moving.

When he dies, pick up the reward where he fell.

Van Horsing's reward

Check · 4-1, back layer · needs Van Horsing's rescue and Vlad

What it is

The vampire hunter you freed in Volcana turns up again in the Temple, cursed and sure he hasn't long left. He hands you what he's carrying, the Alien Compass in the base game, which finds the Mothership in the Ice Caves. In Shell Game he hands you this check's reward instead, and the Alien Compass is shuffled to a check somewhere in the run.

How to reach it

This check starts in Volcana, so a run that went through the Jungle can't get it. Van Horsing only comes to the Temple if you:

  1. freed him from his cell on 2-1, the Van Horsing's rescue check, and
  2. killed Vlad with Van Horsing there at the top of the castle, whichever of you landed the blow.

If you did both, he's waiting in a room in the back layer of 4-1.

How to do it

  1. Find the door into the back layer on 4-1 and the room he's in.
  2. Walk up to him. He gives you the reward.

Stars Challenge

Check · 4-2 · also in the Tide Pool

What it is

Another of Tun's paid challenges: a dark room full of unlit torches, and a short time to light every one. In the base game the Temple version pays out an Elixir. In Shell Game it pays this check's reward instead, and the Elixir is shuffled to a check somewhere in the run.

The Stars Challenge comes in a Temple version and a Tide Pool version. They are separate checks, but a run only ever passes through one of the two areas.

How to reach it

Tun stands outside the challenge's door on 4-2. Talk to her and pay the entry fee, $25,000 in the base game, and the way in opens.

Shell Game always gives you a way to fly or glide before this check: one of the Cape, Vlad's Cape or the Jetpack is always placed earlier in the run. Bring it.

How to do it

  1. Take the torch, and wear your cape or jetpack.
  2. Light every torch in the room before time runs out. Work around the outside of the room first, then the middle, so you don't have to double back.
  3. Keep hold of your torch. If you throw it, you lose time going to pick it up again.
  4. Once every torch is lit, collect the reward.

If Tun is angry with you or dead, the challenge is closed for the run.

The City of Gold

Secret level · from 4-2 · needs the Scepter, and the Crown or the Hedjet

What it is

A hidden level built entirely of gold, which takes the place of 4-3. It holds no check of its own, but it is the only way into the Duat, and so to Anubis II and Osiris. Expect golden crush traps that blend into the walls, mummies, crocodile men and leprechauns, and more gold than you can carry.

How to reach it

On 4-2 there's a door of solid gold. It opens only for a player with the Scepter who also has the Crown or the Hedjet. Shell Game always makes all of these available before you need them here: the Scepter on a check outside the Tide Pool, and one of the Crown or the Hedjet as well. Watch for the crush traps near the door.

How to do it

  1. Wear the Crown or the Hedjet, carry the Scepter to the golden door, and use it to open the door. Go through.
  2. Make your way to the altar near the middle of the level. It's one of Kali's altars, but with a golden bull in place of her statue, an Ankh on its forehead.
  3. If you're going on to the Duat, carry the Ankh and get yourself sacrificed on that altar: let yourself be knocked out while you lie across it, for instance by dropping onto it from a height. Kali takes you, the Ankh is used up, and you wake in the Duat.

Not everything goes with you. With Shell Game's Kali Item Recovery option on, what you're holding and wearing when you're sacrificed, and durable gear you've set down by the altar (a sword, a gun, the Scepter, the Eggplant and the like), waits for you on the Duat's altar instead of being lost. See the options.

Without the Ankh, dying on the altar is just dying. Leave by the level's ordinary exit instead and 4-4 is an ordinary Temple level.

The Duat

Secret level · from the City of Gold · needs the Ankh

What it is

The land of the dead, which takes the place of 4-4. Unlike every other level, you start at the bottom and climb to the top, where Osiris and his son Anubis II wait to judge you. Both are checks.

How to reach it

Be sacrificed with the Ankh on the bull altar in the City of Gold. Shell Game always makes the Ankh available before Osiris and Anubis II.

How to do it

  1. You arrive with 4 health and without your back item. If Kali Item Recovery is on, the items you brought are waiting on Kali's altar here.
  2. Climb. The level has no side walls, so ropes may find nothing to catch on. Snakes, cobras and Ammit roam it, and the black smoke at either edge stuns you and throws you back.
  3. Apep, a giant serpent, passes through twice on the way up. Touching its body hurts and stuns you; its head kills you outright. Get out of its path.
  4. Kali's altar is near the top, just under Apep's second pass. You can't sacrifice anything on it, but it rewards the favor you've already built with Kali. With Shell Game's Balanced Duat Kali Rewards option on, the rewards are bags of bombs and ropes, and Royal Jelly at the top, to win back the health you lost getting here.
  5. At the top, Osiris appears, and Anubis II with him.

Anubis II

Check · the Duat · needs the Scepter, the Ankh, and the Crown or the Hedjet

What it is

Osiris's son, who joins the fight at the top of the Duat. In the base game he always drops a Jetpack. In Shell Game he drops this check's reward instead, and the Jetpack is shuffled to a check somewhere in the run.

How to reach it

Climb to the top of the Duat. He appears there shortly after Osiris.

How to do it

  1. Kill him before you take on Osiris. He summons coffins full of enemies, which are more of a threat than Osiris's fists.
  2. A single bomb kills him. Paste makes it stick, so it can't bounce away.
  3. Pick up the reward where he fell.

Osiris

Check · boss of the Duat · needs the Scepter, the Ankh, and the Crown or the Hedjet

What it is

The judge of the dead: a huge floating head with two great fists, at the top of the Duat. In the base game he drops the Tablet of Destiny. In Shell Game he drops this check's reward instead, and the Tablet is shuffled to a check somewhere in the run.

How to reach it

Climb to the top of the Duat. He appears when you get there, with Anubis II.

How to do it

  1. Deal with Anubis II first.
  2. Osiris drifts back and forth and brings a fist down whenever you pass underneath it. A slam costs 2 health and breaks the floor; running into the side of a fist stuns you and knocks you back. Touching him costs no health, but it knocks you off balance.
  3. Only his face takes damage, and he has 40 health. Bait a fist into slamming, jump onto it, and either whip his face as he drifts past or throw bombs at it. About four bombs with Paste on them does it; bombs that stick to his fists don't reach his face. Jumping on his head does nothing unless you're wearing Spike Shoes, which make it hurt.
  4. He bursts into emeralds when he dies. Grab the reward quickly: depending on where he dies, it can fall into the levels below, where Apep, enemies and lava are waiting.
  5. A portal opens where he was. It leads to the Ice Caves.

Keep away from the black smoke at either side of the arena: it stuns you and can throw you down into Apep's part of the level.
