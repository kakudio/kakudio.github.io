Tiamat's Throne

Tiamat's Throne is level 6-4, straight after Neo Babylon. It is one great hall: Tiamat sits on her throne in the middle, and under the hall is deep water, not floor.

Its one check is Tiamat herself. Far above her throne, past a shaft of laser walls and spark traps, is the way on to the Sunken City, which a run to the Cosmic Ocean has to take.

In Shell Game the check holds whatever your seed put there.

Tiamat

Check · boss · 6-4

What it is

The queen of the Olmites, and the end of a normal run of Spelunky 2. In the base game she drops nothing, and beating her opens the door beside her throne to the game's ending. In Shell Game, beating her also earns this check's reward, which appears once she has finished dying.

How to reach it

Leave 6-3 through its exit and you're in her hall. You can stand on the armrests of her throne and on her right shoulder; her face is shielded by two tall columns of blocks, one on each side of her head, which stop shots from a distance.

Stay off the water. Tentacles reach up out of it and drag down anyone who comes too close, which kills you, and anything that falls into it is gone for good. Bubbles rise from the open water now and then, and you can stand on them; landing on one never hurts.

How to do it

She has 40 health, and anything hurts her, even the whip. Her attacks:

  • Her staff calls up floating red orbs. Hit one in time and it's gone; leave it and it turns into a monster: a snake, a bat, a caveman, a yeti, a UFO and others.
  • Attack her from close by and she roars, for 1 damage and a stun, which can knock you off your footing. She can't roar again for about seven seconds, so a roar is your moment to get back in.

Ways to finish her, from easiest:

  • Teleport into her. Stand right under her and use a Teleporter or Telepack to land inside her, keeping as close to the ground as you can. It kills her outright; if it misses, try again.
  • Freeze Ray. Freeze her head and hit it: two blows finish her.
  • Shotgun. Bomb away the column of blocks on one side of her face, throw a rope up the wall beside her, and shoot her face from the rope. A Plasma Cannon works the same way. Tusk's bodyguards in Neo Babylon carry shotguns.
  • Bombs. Set several bombs quickly on a column beside her head and whip them into her face before they go off.
  • The whip alone. Whip her and throw the bodies of the monsters she calls up, dodging each roar and using a rope to climb back to her face. It's slow, but the ghost never comes to this level, so you can take your time.

Pick up the reward once she's gone. Don't take the door beside her throne unless you mean to end the run there: the Cosmic Ocean is the other way, up past the lasers to the Sunken City. The Qilin, which hatches on 6-3 from the Ushabti the Tablet of Destiny marks on 6-2, flies you up there most easily.
