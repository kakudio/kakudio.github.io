Tide Pool

A Shell Game guide to the checks in the Tide Pool and Abzu

The Tide Pool's checks

The Tide Pool is levels 4-1 to 4-4: pagodas and coral over deep water. It is one of the two ways on from Olmec's Lair; the other is the Temple. You take the Tide Pool through the door at the right of Olmec's middle floor, the one marked by crabs, and a run that does never sees the Temple, so its checks wait for another run.

Its checks are among the hardest in the run. The Dice House and the Idol Heist are on 4-1 (or 4-3, see below); Excalibur's stone, Great Humphead, his cave Idol and the Stars Challenge are all on 4-2; and Kingu waits in Abzu, a hidden level you reach from the bottom of 4-3. Abzu takes the place of 4-4, and either way the area ends at the Ice Caves.

In Shell Game each check here holds whatever your seed put there, not the item the base game gives.

Madame Tusk's Dice House

Check · 4-1, or 4-3

What it is

Madame Tusk runs a gambling den in the Tide Pool, watched over by a bodyguard with a shotgun. You pay to throw two dice: roll a seven and you win the prize on display behind a force field, and once you've won five prizes the house has nothing left to give you. In Shell Game the first four prizes are the ordinary shop items the base game gives; the fifth, last prize is this check's reward.

Winning all five still earns you Tusk's invitation to her Palace of Pleasure in Neo Babylon, as it does in the base game, which opens the Madame Tusk's Palace check.

How to reach it

The Dice House is usually on 4-1, a building on the main path with Tusk and her bodyguard inside. If Sparrow's hideout is on 4-1, which happens when you robbed a shop late in the Jungle or Volcana, the Dice House moves to 4-3 instead.

How to do it

  1. Bring money: each game costs $5,000. Pay Tusk, pick up the two dice and throw them onto the table.
  2. A total of seven wins the prize on display. Eight or more pays you $10,000, more than the game costs, so a lucky run of high rolls pays for the next few tries; six or less wins nothing.
  3. Keep playing until you've won five prizes. The fifth is this check's reward.

Play fair. Throwing a die a second time in the same game, carrying a die out of the house or damaging the house turns Tusk and her bodyguard against you. Don't kill Tusk either: she drops a key, but with her dead the Palace of Pleasure never appears, and the Neo Babylon checks that depend on it are gone for the run.

Idol Heist

Check · 4-1 or 4-3, by the Dice House · needs a Skeleton Key · part of Sparrow's quest

What it is

Madame Tusk keeps her golden Idol in a small locked room beside the Dice House. Stealing it is the second step of Sparrow's quest: she dares you to rob Tusk when you first meet her, and getting the Idol out of the level is what brings her back later in Neo Babylon. In Shell Game the Idol's spot holds this check's reward instead, and Madame Tusk's Idol is shuffled to a check somewhere in the run.

The rest of Sparrow's quest is on the anywhere page. This entry is the heist itself.

How to reach it

The locked room is just outside Tusk's Dice House, on whichever level the house is on, 4-1 or 4-3. In the base game the room holds the Idol only if you've met Sparrow earlier in the run. If you haven't, Sparrow got there first and all you find is her note, so start her quest before the Tide Pool:

  1. Steal from a shop before Olmec's Lair and get away with it: carry an unpaid item out of the shop so the shopkeeper shouts "thief", not "vandal".
  2. On a later level, from 2-1 at the earliest to 4-1 at the latest, look for a small wooden hut leading into the back layer. Sparrow is in there. Walk up to her and she throws you a pile of ropes (the Shoplifter check) and challenges you to the heist. Don't skip her level: miss her and her quest is over.
  3. Go on from Olmec's Lair to the Tide Pool, not the Temple.

The room's door is locked. A Skeleton Key opens it, and Shell Game always makes one available before you need it here. Any other key you find on the level opens it too.

How to do it

  1. Plan your way out before you open the door. If Tusk or her bodyguard sees you near the room with what you took, or you walk too close to them carrying it, both turn on you, and the bodyguard's shotgun hits hard. Find a route to the level exit that keeps well away from the Dice House; bombs can open one.
  2. Open the locked door with the key and take the reward.
  3. Carry it out through the level exit without going back past Tusk.

Never kill Tusk. Her key would open the room, but with her dead Sparrow's quest can't go on and the Palace of Pleasure never appears. Being caught and chased doesn't end the quest, as long as Tusk lives.

In Shell Game, the Idol itself moves Sparrow's quest along whenever you carry it through a level exit, wherever your seed put it. Shell Game places it no earlier than the Tide Pool and no later than Neo Babylon, and that includes Madame Tusk's Palace and Tusk's Vault on 6-3. Those come after Sparrow's meeting on 6-1, so on a seed that puts the Idol there, her quest may not reach the vault. If this heist gives you something else, keep an eye out for the Idol: it doesn't have to come from Tusk's room to count.

Excalibur's stone

Check · 4-2 · needs the Crown or the Hedjet

What it is

A sword set in a stone, low in 4-2. In the base game it's Excalibur, which only a player wearing a crown can pull free: a sword that kills most things in one swing and the one weapon that cracks Kingu's shell. In Shell Game the stone holds this check's reward instead, and Excalibur is shuffled to a check somewhere in the run.

How to reach it

Every 4-2 has the stone, on the ledge just above the water of the Great Lake at the bottom of the level. You need the Crown or the Hedjet on your head when you get there; without one, the stone gives you nothing.

How to do it

  1. Wear the Crown or the Hedjet into 4-2.
  2. Work your way down to the lake. The stone sits just above the water line.
  3. Take the reward from the stone.

The lake below is Great Humphead's, so you can do both checks on one trip down.

Great Humphead's cave Idol

Check · 4-2, under the Great Lake, back layer

What it is

A golden Idol inside a giant clam, in a wrecked wooden building at the bottom of the Great Lake. In the base game it is ordinary treasure, worth a good deal of money. In Shell Game the clam holds this check's reward instead.

How to reach it

On 4-2, swim down to the bottom of the Great Lake, below Excalibur's stone. The entrance to the sunken building is there, and it leads into the back layer, where the clam waits along with spikes, treasure and the remains of an unlucky caveman. Great Humphead patrols the same water, so deal with him first or move quickly.

How to do it

  1. Get into the sunken building and find the clam.
  2. Take the reward from it on your first try. The clam snaps shut when you reach in, and if you don't come away with the reward, it swallows it and the check is lost.
  3. Watch the spikes on the way out.

Great Humphead

Check · boss of the Great Lake, 4-2

What it is

A huge fish that lives in the Great Lake. When he dies he leaves three things: a hired hand, a Camera and a present. In the base game the present is your one reliable source of a gift for Kali, who turns it into the Eggplant. In Shell Game the present is replaced by this check's reward; the hired hand and the Camera stay as they are, and the Eggplant is shuffled to a check somewhere in the run.

How to reach it

Every 4-2 has him, in the Great Lake at the bottom of the level, just below Excalibur's stone.

How to do it

  1. He charges at you whenever you swim into range, and touching him costs 2 health. After a while he backs off, then comes again. He has 40 health, a lot for your whip.
  2. Find somewhere he can't reach but you can still hit him from. The doorway of the sunken building at the bottom of the lake is one such spot.
  3. Hit him with something heavier than the whip: bombs, a Shotgun or a sword. A Camera's flash makes him lose interest and turn away for a moment, which buys you time to get clear.
  4. When he dies, collect the reward where the present would have been. It's underwater, where he fell.

The hired hand he drops follows you if you keep him alive, and can carry a heavy item for you.

Stars Challenge

Check · 4-2 · also in the Temple

What it is

Another of Tun's paid challenges: a dark room full of unlit torches, and a short time to light every one. In the base game the Tide Pool version pays out a Clone Gun. In Shell Game it pays this check's reward instead, and the Clone Gun is shuffled to a check somewhere in the run.

The Stars Challenge comes in a Tide Pool version and a Temple version. They are separate checks, but a run only ever passes through one of the two areas.

How to reach it

Tun stands outside the challenge's door on 4-2. Talk to her and pay the entry fee, $25,000 in the base game, and the way in opens.

Shell Game always gives you a way to fly or glide before this check: one of the Cape, Vlad's Cape or the Jetpack is always placed earlier in the run. Bring it.

How to do it

  1. Take the torch, and wear your cape or jetpack.
  2. Light every torch in the room before time runs out. Work around the outside of the room first, then the middle, so you don't have to double back.
  3. Keep hold of your torch. If you throw it, you lose time going to pick it up again.
  4. Once every torch is lit, collect the reward.

If Tun is angry with you or dead, the challenge is closed for the run.

Abzu

Secret level · from 4-3 · needs the Ankh

What it is

A hidden level that takes the place of 4-4, and the home of Kingu. Its door is sunk deep in a lake of lava at the bottom of 4-3, and the only way to drain the lava is a trap that almost always kills whoever sets it off. That's what the Ankh is for: it brings you back, and the door is open when you return. Shell Game always makes the Ankh available before Kingu.

How to reach it

On 4-3, a room on the main path always leads into a back-layer passage that winds down to the trap at the bottom of the level. Two torches mark the Abzu door in the lava, which helps on a dark level.

How to do it

  1. Before you go down, leave anything you want to keep somewhere safe in the upper part of 4-3, especially the sword you'll need against Kingu. Whatever you're holding when you die is lost to the lava.
  2. Follow the back-layer passage down and set off the trap. The lava drains, and you'll almost certainly die.
  3. The Ankh brings you back to life in the upper part of the level. It's used up.
  4. Collect what you left behind, go back down to the drained lake and through the door into Abzu.

Kingu

Check · boss of Abzu · needs the Ankh and Excalibur

What it is

A giant sea creature in a hard shell, clinging to the walls of Abzu. In the base game she drops the Tablet of Destiny. In Shell Game she drops this check's reward instead, and the Tablet is shuffled to a check somewhere in the run.

How to reach it

Go through Abzu's door at the bottom of 4-3, which takes the Ankh. Kingu crawls slowly around the walls of the level. Bring Excalibur: Shell Game always places it before Kingu, and never in the Temple, so it's always somewhere you can get to on the way here.

How to do it

  1. Get onto the platform on Kingu's back by dropping onto it from above. Climb up through the area in the lower part of Abzu to get above her, or fly there if you can.
  2. While you stand on her back she calls up enemies, three at a time, every few seconds: octopuses and hopping jiangshi. Deal with them as they come, or keep moving.
  3. Nothing hurts her until her shell cracks. One swing of Excalibur cracks it; without the sword it takes about ten bombs' worth of explosions, and loose bombs tend to fall through the platform, so Paste helps.
  4. Once the shell is open, keep hitting her until she dies. She slides down to the bottom of the level and turns to stone.
  5. Pick up the reward where she ends up, then take the way on to the Ice Caves.
