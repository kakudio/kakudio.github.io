Tide Pool

A guide to the Tide Pool and Abzu, for players of Shell Game

The Tide Pool

The Tide Pool is levels 4-1 to 4-4: pagodas and coral over deep water. It is one of the two ways on from Olmec's Lair; the other is the Temple. You take the Tide Pool through the door at the right of Olmec's middle floor, the one marked by crabs, and a run that does never sees the Temple.

Much of what's here is among the hardest things in a run. Madame Tusk's Dice House, with her Idol locked up beside it, is on 4-1, or on 4-3 when Sparrow has taken over 4-1. Every 4-2 has the Great Lake at the bottom of the level, with Excalibur's stone on its shore, Great Humphead in its water and a sunken Idol beneath it; Tun's Stars Challenge is on 4-2 as well. From the bottom of 4-3 a hidden door leads to Abzu, Kingu's home, which takes the place of 4-4. Either way the area ends at the Ice Caves.

Madame Tusk's Dice House and the Idol Heist

Madame Tusk runs a gambling den in the Tide Pool, watched over by a bodyguard with a shotgun, and keeps her golden Idol in a small locked room just outside it. The Dice House is usually on 4-1, a building on the main path with Tusk and her bodyguard inside. If Sparrow's hideout is on 4-1, which happens when you robbed a shop late in the Jungle or Volcana, the Dice House moves to 4-3 instead, and the Idol's room goes with it.

You pay to throw two dice: roll a seven and you win the prize on display behind a force field, and once you've won five prizes the house has nothing left to give you. Winning all five earns you Tusk's invitation to her Palace of Pleasure in Neo Babylon, Madame Tusk's Palace.

  1. Bring money: each game costs $5,000. Pay Tusk, pick up the two dice and throw them onto the table.
  2. A total of seven wins the prize on display. Eight or more pays you $10,000, more than the game costs, so a lucky run of high rolls pays for the next few tries; six or less wins nothing.
  3. Keep playing until you've won five prizes.

Play fair. Throwing a die a second time in the same game, carrying a die out of the house or damaging the house turns Tusk and her bodyguard against you. Don't kill Tusk either: she drops a key, but with her dead the Palace of Pleasure never appears, and Sparrow's quest can't go on.

In Shell Game The first four prizes are the ordinary shop items the base game gives. Only the fifth, last prize is a check, and winning it still earns Tusk's invitation to the Palace.

The Idol Heist

Stealing Tusk's Idol is the second step of Sparrow's quest: she dares you to rob Tusk when you first meet her, and getting the Idol out of the level is what brings her back later in Neo Babylon. The rest of her quest is on the anywhere page; this is the heist itself.

The locked room holds the Idol only if you've met Sparrow earlier in the run. If you haven't, Sparrow got there first and all you find is her note, so start her quest before the Tide Pool:

  1. Steal from a shop before Olmec's Lair and get away with it: carry an unpaid item out of the shop so the shopkeeper shouts "thief", not "vandal".
  2. On a later level, from 2-1 at the earliest to 4-1 at the latest, look for a small wooden hut leading into the back layer. Sparrow is in there. Walk up to her and she throws you a pile of ropes (the Shoplifter check) and challenges you to the heist. Don't skip her level: miss her and her quest is over.
  3. Go on from Olmec's Lair to the Tide Pool, not the Temple.

The room's door is locked. A Skeleton Key opens it, and so does any other key you find on the level. Then:

  1. Plan your way out before you open the door. If Tusk or her bodyguard sees you near the room with what you took, or you walk too close to them carrying it, both turn on you, and the bodyguard's shotgun hits hard. Find a route to the level exit that keeps well away from the Dice House; bombs can open one.
  2. Open the locked door with the key and take the Idol.
  3. Carry it out through the level exit without going back past Tusk.

Never kill Tusk for her key. With her dead, Sparrow's quest can't go on and the Palace of Pleasure never appears. Being caught and chased doesn't end the quest, as long as Tusk lives.

In Shell Game A Skeleton Key is always somewhere you can get it before the heist. The Idol's spot in the room holds an item from your run, and Madame Tusk's Idol can turn up anywhere from the Tide Pool to Neo Babylon. Wherever it comes from, carrying it through a level exit moves Sparrow's quest on, so if this heist gives you something else, keep an eye out for the Idol. Its latest spots include Madame Tusk's Palace and Tusk's Vault on 6-3, which come after Sparrow's meeting on 6-1, so on a seed that puts the Idol there her quest may not reach the vault.

Excalibur's stone

Every 4-2 has a sword set in a stone, on the ledge just above the water of the Great Lake at the bottom of the level. It's Excalibur, a sword that kills most things in one swing and the one weapon that cracks Kingu's shell, and only a player wearing a crown can pull it free: the Crown or the Hedjet. Without one, the stone gives you nothing.

Wear the Crown or the Hedjet into 4-2, work your way down to the lake, and take what the stone holds. The lake below is Great Humphead's, so you can deal with him and his Idol on the same trip down.

Great Humphead and his cave Idol

Great Humphead is a huge fish that lives in the Great Lake, at the bottom of every 4-2, just below Excalibur's stone. When he dies he leaves three things: a hired hand, a Camera and a present. The present is the base game's one reliable gift for Kali, who turns it into the Eggplant.

  1. He charges at you whenever you swim into range, and touching him costs 2 health. After a while he backs off, then comes again. He has 40 health, a lot for your whip.
  2. Find somewhere he can't reach but you can still hit him from. The doorway of the sunken building at the bottom of the lake is one such spot.
  3. Hit him with something heavier than the whip: bombs, a Shotgun or a sword. A Camera's flash makes him lose interest and turn away for a moment, which buys you time to get clear.
  4. When he dies, collect what he drops. It's underwater, where he fell.

The hired hand he drops follows you if you keep him alive, and can carry a heavy item for you.

In Shell Game In place of the present he leaves an item from your run, so he gives you no gift for Kali; Shell Game sets out its own Present for her. The hired hand and the Camera are the base game's.

The cave Idol

At the bottom of the Great Lake lies a wrecked wooden building, its doorway the same one that makes a good spot to fight Humphead from. It leads into the back layer, where a giant clam holds a golden Idol, alongside spikes, treasure and the remains of an unlucky caveman. In the base game the Idol is ordinary treasure, worth a good deal of money. Humphead patrols the water outside, so deal with him first or move quickly.

  1. Get into the sunken building and find the clam.
  2. Take the Idol on your first try. The clam snaps shut when you reach in, and if you don't come away with it, the clam swallows it and it's gone.
  3. Watch the spikes on the way out.

In Shell Game The clam holds an item from your run instead of the Idol, so it's worth the risk of the clam's bite for more than money.

Stars Challenge

Another of Tun's paid challenges: a dark room full of unlit torches, and a short time to light every one. The Tide Pool's version pays out a Clone Gun. Tun stands outside the challenge's door on 4-2. Talk to her and pay the entry fee, $25,000 in the base game, and the way in opens.

The Temple has its own Stars Challenge, but a run only ever passes through one of the two areas.

  1. Take the torch, and wear a cape or a jetpack if you have one.
  2. Light every torch in the room before time runs out. Work around the outside of the room first, then the middle, so you don't have to double back.
  3. Keep hold of your torch. If you throw it, you lose time going to pick it up again.
  4. Once every torch is lit, collect the prize.

If Tun is angry with you or dead, the challenge is closed for the run.

In Shell Game You always get a way to fly or glide before this challenge: one of the Cape, Vlad's Cape or the Jetpack is always placed earlier in the run. Bring it. The Tide Pool's and the Temple's Stars Challenges are separate checks.

Abzu and Kingu

Abzu is a hidden level that takes the place of 4-4, and the home of Kingu. Its door is sunk deep in a lake of lava at the bottom of 4-3, and the only way to drain the lava is a trap that almost always kills whoever sets it off. That's what the Ankh is for: it brings you back, and the door is open when you return.

On 4-3, a room on the main path always leads into a back-layer passage that winds down to the trap at the bottom of the level. Two torches mark the Abzu door in the lava, which helps on a dark level.

  1. Before you go down, leave anything you want to keep somewhere safe in the upper part of 4-3, especially the sword you'll need against Kingu. Whatever you're holding when you die is lost to the lava.
  2. Follow the back-layer passage down and set off the trap. The lava drains, and you'll almost certainly die.
  3. The Ankh brings you back to life in the upper part of the level. It's used up.
  4. Collect what you left behind, go back down to the drained lake and through the door into Abzu.

In Shell Game The Ankh is always somewhere you can get it before Kingu.

Kingu

A giant sea creature in a hard shell, clinging to the walls of Abzu and crawling slowly around them. When she dies she drops the Tablet of Destiny. Bring Excalibur.

  1. Get onto the platform on Kingu's back by dropping onto it from above. Climb up through the area in the lower part of Abzu to get above her, or fly there if you can.
  2. While you stand on her back she calls up enemies, three at a time, every few seconds: octopuses and hopping jiangshi. Deal with them as they come, or keep moving.
  3. Nothing hurts her until her shell cracks. One swing of Excalibur cracks it; without the sword it takes about ten bombs' worth of explosions, and loose bombs tend to fall through the platform, so Paste helps.
  4. Once the shell is open, keep hitting her until she dies. She slides down to the bottom of the level and turns to stone.
  5. Pick up her drop where she ends up, then take the way on to the Ice Caves.

In Shell Game Excalibur may not be in its stone, but it's always placed before Kingu and never in the Temple, so it's always somewhere you can get to on the way here.
