A Shell Game guide to the checks in Volcana

Volcana's checks

Volcana is levels 2-1 to 2-4. It is one of the two ways from the Dwelling to Olmec; the other is the Jungle. You take Volcana by leaving 1-4 through its right exit, and a run that does never sees the Jungle, so its checks wait for another run.

In Shell Game each check here holds whatever your seed put there, not the item the base game gives.

  • Moon Challenge
  • Van Horsing's rescue
  • Vlad
  • Vlad's Castle

Moon Challenge

Check · 2-2, 2-3 or 2-4 · also in the Jungle

What it is

One of Tun's paid challenges: a dark room packed with earth, where you dig for a prize with a Mattock. In the base game the prize is Hou Yi's Bow, sealed in a pocket of brick. In Shell Game the Bow is shuffled to a check somewhere in the run, and the pocket holds this check's reward. The Metal Arrow that comes with the Bow is gone too.

The Moon Challenge comes in a Volcana version and a Jungle version. They are separate checks, but a run only ever passes through one of the two areas.

How to reach it

Tun stands outside the challenge's door on one of 2-2, 2-3 or 2-4. Talk to her and pay the entry fee, $10,000 in the base game, and the way in opens.

How to do it

  1. Pick up the Mattock Tun gives you. It breaks after a limited number of swings, so dig with purpose rather than clearing the whole room.
  2. Dig down. The reward's brick pocket is in the lower part of the room. Watch for scorpions and arrow traps in the dark.
  3. Break into the pocket and take the reward.

Don't break the platform or the force fields at the entrance, and don't dig into the challenge from above: either one angers Tun.

Van Horsing's rescue

Check · 2-1, back layer

What it is

Van Horsing, a vampire hunter, has been locked in a cell on the first level of Volcana. Let him out and he thanks you with a Diamond in the base game; in Shell Game, with this check's reward.

Freeing him matters beyond this check. He follows you to Vlad and deals with him for you, and if he does, he turns up again in the Temple with another reward: the Van Horsing's reward check.

How to reach it

On 2-1, his cell is in the back layer. The key to it is somewhere else on the same level. A level can have more than one cell but only one key, so make sure you're opening his.

How to do it

  1. Find the key and carry it to Van Horsing's cell.
  2. Open the cell and talk to him.
  3. Pick up the reward he leaves.

Vlad

Check · boss of Vlad's Castle · needs the Udjat Eye

What it is

The vampire lord who rests at the top of his castle, just above the statue that holds the Crown. In the base game he drops Vlad's Cape when he dies. In Shell Game he drops this check's reward instead, and Vlad's Cape is shuffled to a check somewhere in the run.

How to reach it

He's at the very top of Vlad's Castle, so getting to him is the same climb as getting to the Crown.

How to do it

If you freed and talked to Van Horsing on 2-1, he's waiting at the top of the castle. Once Vlad wakes, Van Horsing shoots him with a Metal Arrow, which kills him at once. Pick up the reward where Vlad fell.

Without Van Horsing you have to fight him:

  • He behaves like an ordinary vampire, flying at you, but teleports away from your attacks, with a short pause after each jump. He has 10 health and heals himself by drinking blood.
  • A Metal Arrow, a lit arrow or the flash of a Camera kills him instantly.
  • A Freeze Ray freezes him, and he can be hurt while he's frozen.

Vlad's Castle

Check · 2-2, 2-3 or 2-4, back layer · needs the Udjat Eye

What it is

A vampire's castle buried deep in Volcana's back layer. At the top, on a statue, sits the Crown in the base game. In Shell Game the statue holds this check's reward, and the Crown is shuffled to a check somewhere in the run.

How to reach it

You need the Udjat Eye; Shell Game always makes it available before Vlad's Castle. On the level with the castle, one of 2-2, 2-3 or 2-4, a huge drill sits along the top of the level.

  1. Bring the Udjat Eye to the drill to set it going. It bores straight down through the level and leaves a chain you can climb.
  2. Follow the shaft to the bottom of the level, where a door in a red brick building leads into the back layer and the castle.

How to do it

  1. Climb the castle from the bottom. Expect coffins, conveyor belts and a pit of spikes low down, and arrow traps further up. A room behind sliding walls in the middle holds treasure, but also the level's cursed pot.
  2. At the top, take the reward from the statue.
  3. Vlad rests just above it, and is a check of his own.
