Volcana

A guide to Volcana, for players of Shell Game

Volcana

Volcana is levels 2-1 to 2-4. It is one of the two ways from the Dwelling to Olmec; the other is the Jungle. You take Volcana by leaving 1-4 through its right exit, and a run that does never sees the Jungle.

Its story starts on 2-1, where a vampire hunter is locked in a cell, and leads to Vlad's Castle, buried in the back layer of one of the later levels. One of 2-2, 2-3 or 2-4 also has Tun's Moon Challenge.

Van Horsing

Van Horsing, a vampire hunter, has been locked in a cell in the back layer of 2-1. The key to it is somewhere else on the same level. A level can have more than one cell but only one key, so make sure you're opening his.

  1. Find the key and carry it to Van Horsing's cell.
  2. Open the cell and talk to him.
  3. He thanks you with a Diamond. Pick it up where he leaves it.

Freeing him matters beyond the Diamond. He follows you to Vlad and deals with him for you, and if he does, he turns up again in the Temple with another reward.

Vlad's Castle and Vlad

A vampire's castle is buried deep in Volcana's back layer, on one of 2-2, 2-3 or 2-4. At the top, on a statue, sits the Crown, and just above it rests Vlad himself. Getting there takes the Udjat Eye: on the castle's level a huge drill sits along the top of the level, and the Eye sets it going.

  1. Bring the Udjat Eye to the drill. It bores straight down through the level and leaves a chain you can climb.
  2. Follow the shaft to the bottom of the level, where a door in a red brick building leads into the back layer and the castle.
  3. Climb the castle from the bottom. Expect coffins, conveyor belts and a pit of spikes low down, and arrow traps further up. A room behind sliding walls in the middle holds treasure, but also the level's cursed pot.
  4. At the top, take the Crown from the statue.

In Shell Game The Udjat Eye is always somewhere you can get it before Vlad's Castle.

Vlad

The vampire lord rests just above the statue. When he dies he drops Vlad's Cape.

If you freed and talked to Van Horsing on 2-1, he's waiting at the top of the castle. Once Vlad wakes, Van Horsing shoots him with a Metal Arrow, which kills him at once. Pick up the Cape where Vlad fell.

Without Van Horsing you have to fight him:

  • He behaves like an ordinary vampire, flying at you, but teleports away from your attacks, with a short pause after each jump. He has 10 health and heals himself by drinking blood.
  • A Metal Arrow, a lit arrow or the flash of a Camera kills him instantly.
  • A Freeze Ray freezes him, and he can be hurt while he's frozen.

Moon Challenge

One of Tun's paid challenges: a dark room packed with earth, where you dig with a Mattock for Hou Yi's Bow and the Metal Arrow that comes with it, sealed in a pocket of brick. Tun stands outside the challenge's door on one of 2-2, 2-3 or 2-4. Talk to her and pay the entry fee, $10,000 in the base game, and the way in opens.

The Jungle has its own Moon Challenge, but a run only ever passes through one of the two areas.

  1. Pick up the Mattock Tun gives you. It breaks after a limited number of swings, so dig with purpose rather than clearing the whole room.
  2. Dig down. The brick pocket is in the lower part of the room. Watch for scorpions and arrow traps in the dark.
  3. Break into the pocket and take the prize.

Don't break the platform or the force fields at the entrance, and don't dig into the challenge from above: either one angers Tun.

In Shell Game The brick pocket holds an item from your run, and the Metal Arrow that comes with the Bow is gone too, so it can't be your answer to Vlad. The Jungle's and Volcana's Moon Challenges are separate checks.
